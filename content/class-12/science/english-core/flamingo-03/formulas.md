# Deep Water — value points

:::formula Deep Water — plot in six beats
Beach wave at 3–4 · YMCA pool at 10–11 · bully throws him in, three attempts, blackout · years of terror near water · instructor, Oct–April, belt-rope-pulley, breathing, kicking · Lake Wentworth and Warm Lake, fear conquered
:::

:::formula Origin of the fear — value points
California beach, waves knocked him down, buried in water, father laughed · YMCA pool chosen as safe · bruiser tossed him into 9 ft · near-drowning · aversion became terror
:::

:::formula Near-drowning — value points
Landed sitting, swallowed water · plan: jump, surface, lie flat, paddle · 9 ft felt like 90 · yellowish water, nothing to grab · second jump: paralysed legs, no scream · third: effort ceased, peace, darkness · revived vomiting
:::

:::formula Years of handicap — value points
Haunting fear whenever near water · legs paralysed, icy horror · no fishing, canoeing, boating, swimming · Cascades, Tieton, Bumping, Warm Lake, Columbia spoiled
:::

:::formula Instructor's method — value points
Five days a week, one hour · belt, rope, pulley on overhead cable, three months · face under water, exhale; nose up, inhale · leg kicks at the pool side · pieces combined · October to April
:::

:::formula Self-tests — value points
Talked back to the vestige of fear in the pool · Lake Wentworth: Triggs Island to Stamp Act Island, two miles, laughed at the fear · Warm Lake: Conrad Meadows, swam across and back, shouted with joy
:::

:::formula Message — value points
Terror and peace in the face of death · death is not the enemy, fear of death is · fear can be defeated by will and method · conquering it gives freedom to live intensely · Roosevelt: fear itself
:::
