# Indigo — value points

:::formula Indigo — plot in six beats
Shukla pursues Gandhi from Lucknow · Patna servants, Muzzafarpur lawyers · 15 per cent indigo clause, synthetic indigo, compensation swindle · Motihari: order to quit, defiance, crowd, trial, lawyers' vow, case dropped · inquiry, 25 per cent refund, planters leave · schools, sanitation, Dr Dev, self-reliance
:::

:::formula Rajkumar Shukla — value points
Poor, thin, illiterate sharecropper · from Champaran · met Gandhi at Lucknow 1916 · followed him to Cawnpore, Ahmedabad, Calcutta · resolute, tenacious · brought Gandhi to Patna and Champaran
:::

:::formula Sharecropping system — value points
English landlords, Indian tenants · indigo on 15 per cent of holding · whole indigo harvest as rent · German synthetic indigo · landlords demand compensation to cancel clause · peasants cheated, want money back
:::

:::formula Motihari defiance — value points
Commissioner bullies · elephant ride to a maltreated peasant · notice to quit · writes he will disobey · thousands around courthouse · officials powerless · Gandhi regulates crowd · guilty plea by conscience · no bail · lawyers vow jail · case dropped
:::

:::formula Settlement — value points
Commission with Gandhi for peasants · evidence overwhelming · landlords agree to refund · Gandhi asks 50 per cent · offered 25, accepts · prestige surrendered, fear broken · planters abandon estates in a few years
:::

:::formula Social uplift — value points
Cultural and social backwardness · appeal for teachers · Mahadev Desai, Narhari Parikh, Devdas · schools in six villages · Kasturba: cleanliness, sanitation · Dr Dev: castor oil, quinine, sulphur ointment
:::

:::formula Lessons of Champaran — value points
First civil disobedience in India · fearlessness of peasants · lawyers' conversion · justice over sum · politics tied to daily life · self-reliance: no Englishman as prop (C. F. Andrews)
:::
