# Aunt Jennifer's Tigers — value points

:::formula Aunt Jennifer's Tigers — the poem in three stanzas
Stanza 1: tigers prance on the screen, bright topaz, world of green, unafraid of men, chivalric certainty · Stanza 2: fingers flutter, ivory needle hard to pull, massive weight of Uncle's wedding band · Stanza 3: dead aunt's terrified hands ringed with ordeals; tigers prance on, proud and unafraid
:::

:::formula The tigers — value points
Embroidered · prance, pace · bright topaz · denizens of a world of green · do not fear men · sleek, chivalric certainty · proud, unafraid · symbol of freedom, strength, the aunt's suppressed self · outlive her
:::

:::formula Aunt Jennifer — value points
Old woman at embroidery · fluttering fingers · needle hard to pull · crushed by wedding band · terrified · mastered by ordeals · no freedom even in death · her spirit lives only in her art
:::

:::formula Wedding band — value points
Uncle's, not hers · massive weight · burden of marriage · male domination · ownership · makes work hard · stays in death · ordeals she was mastered by
:::

:::formula Devices — value points
Symbolism · imagery · alliteration · metaphor (weight) · transferred epithet (terrified hands) · contrast · irony · three quatrains · rhyming couplets aabb
:::

:::formula Themes — value points
Patriarchy · oppressive marriage · fear and lost identity · art as expression and escape · permanence of art · feminist protest without slogans
:::
