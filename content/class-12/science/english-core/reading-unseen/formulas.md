# Unseen passage — method sheet

:::formula Section A at a glance
Q1 unseen passage 12 marks (~450 words, ~10 items) · Q2 case-based/factual passage 10 marks · total 22 · 30–35 minutes · do first
:::

:::formula Two-pass method
Skim: first paragraph, topic sentences, last paragraph, label each paragraph · Read questions, underline keywords · Scan to keyword, read around it · Answer · Check negatives and numbers
:::

:::formula Item types and tricks
Main idea: whole-passage support · Tone: writer's adjectives · Inference: point + evidence · Vocabulary: same part of speech, substitute · True/false: exact clauses · 1-mark: lift the phrase · 2-mark: two sentences, ~40 words
:::

:::formula Tone vocabulary
Critical · nostalgic · humorous · ironic · persuasive · cautionary · reflective · optimistic · matter-of-fact · appreciative
:::

:::formula Function-of-detail phrases
To illustrate the point · to contrast with · to emphasise · to make a statistic concrete · to appeal to the reader's experience · to anticipate an objection
:::
