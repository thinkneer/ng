# A Roadside Stand — value points

:::formula A Roadside Stand — the poem in five moves
Shed by the highway, berries and squash, hope for city money · polished traffic passes; stops only to complain, ask the way, turn, beg gas · childish longing in vain · greedy good-doers plan relocation and dependence · poet's pain and ironic self-check
:::

:::formula The stand and the farmers — value points
Little new shed at the roadside · wild berries in wooden quarts · crook-necked golden squash with silver warts · not bread but city money · flower of cities · open window waiting · squeal of brakes
:::

:::formula The traffic — value points
Polished, shiny cars · mind ahead · selfish cars (transferred epithet) · complain: signs with N and S wrong, shed spoils view · ask the way · turn round in the yard · ask for a gallon of gas · never buy
:::

:::formula Greedy good-doers — value points
Oxymoron, also beneficent beasts of prey · buy out pitiful kin · relocate near theatre and store · no need to think · enforcing benefits · soothe out of their wits · sleep all day, ruin sleep at night · false philanthropy
:::

:::formula Poet's response — value points
Cannot bear childish longing in vain · sadness lurks near window · thought of ending pain at one stroke · turns on himself: would he accept it? · irony · honesty about pity
:::

:::formula Devices and themes — value points
Imagery · alliteration · personification · transferred epithet · oxymoron · metaphor (flower of cities) · irony · rural–urban divide · inequality · false charity · indifference · empathy
:::
