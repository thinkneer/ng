# Going Places — value points

:::formula Going Places — plot in six beats
Sophie's boutique dream vs Jansie's factory · home and Geoff · invented meeting with Danny Casey · father's dismissal, leak to Jansie · waiting by the canal · Saturday match, Casey's goal
:::

:::formula Sophie — value points
Teenager leaving school · dreams: boutique, actress, designer · fascinated by Geoff · invents Casey meeting · half believes it · fears Jansie's gossip · waits by canal · hope persists · dreamer, escapist, lonely
:::

:::formula Jansie — value points
Classmate · realistic, practical · biscuit factory for both · warns Sophie · "nosey", likely to gossip · calls the Casey story nonsense · voice of reality
:::

:::formula Geoff — value points
Three years older · apprentice mechanic · tall, strong, silent · unknown areas of life · Sophie's confidant · sceptical but half-believing · tells Frank · his hope is what Sophie fears to kill
:::

:::formula Danny Casey fantasy — value points
Young Irish star of United · arcade outside Royce's · kind words, new shop · green eyes, not tall · autograph, no paper · promised date next week · lie becomes belief · never comes · scores at the match
:::

:::formula Themes — value points
Fantasy vs reality · hero worship · class and limited opportunity · family: dismissive father, tired mother, silent brother · adolescent longing · persistence of hope · ironic title
:::
