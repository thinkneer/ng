# Article and report — format sheet

:::formula Article — parts
Title · By-line · P1 hook + issue · P2 causes/effects + examples · P3 view + suggestions + closing line · 120–150 words
:::

:::formula Report — parts
HEADLINE · By-line · Place, date: · P1 5 Ws · P2 sequence, highlights, numbers · P3 outcome, winners, response · past tense, passive, factual
:::

:::formula Article / report — marks split (5)
Format 1 (title/headline + by-line) · Content 2 (points asked, examples/5 Ws, sequence, outcome) · Expression 2 (grammar, cohesion, vocabulary, 120–150 words)
:::

:::formula Article on a social problem — value points
Hook · what the problem is · two causes · two effects (health, society, economy) · who is affected · two or three solutions (individual, school, government) · closing line
:::

:::formula Report on a school event — value points
Event name · organiser · date · venue · purpose · inauguration and chief guest · main activities in order · numbers · highlights · winners/prizes · vote of thanks · significance
:::

:::formula Report on an accident / disaster — value points
What happened · where and when · cause (if known) · casualties and damage · rescue and relief by whom · eyewitness response (reported) · present situation · action by authorities
:::
