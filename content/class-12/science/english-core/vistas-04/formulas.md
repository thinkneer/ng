# The Enemy — value points

:::formula The Enemy — plot in six beats
Sadao and Hana find a wounded U.S. Navy POW on the beach · dilemma; carry him in · servants refuse and leave; operation, bullet near kidney · unsent letter; confession to the General; assassins promised · three sleepless nights, nothing · boat, island, flashlight escape; General forgot · why could I not kill him?
:::

:::formula Dr Sadao Hoki — value points
Japanese surgeon · trained in America from 22 · famous, researching clean wounds · kept home for the General · dutiful son, proud, honest · torn between doctor and patriot · saves and frees the enemy · still prejudiced, still humane
:::

:::formula Hana — value points
Met Sadao in America · traditional marriage · fears treason · washes the prisoner herself · assists operation, vomits, returns · manages servants and children · loyal, brave, compassionate
:::

:::formula The dilemma — value points
Shelter = treason · hand over = death · push back = death · surgeon's instinct stops bleeding · decides to operate then report · letter never sent · tells General · lies awake · chooses escape
:::

:::formula The General — value points
Old, ill · trusts only Sadao · pure self-interest · promises private assassins · leave partition open · forgets · admits dereliction, asks silence · irony saves Sadao and Tom
:::

:::formula The escape — value points
Boat at night · food, water, clothes · Japanese dress, black cloth on blond hair · uninhabited, unpatrolled island · Korean fishing boat · flashlight: two = food, one = safe · no signal → gone
:::

:::formula Themes — value points
Humanity above nationality · duty vs conscience · prejudice on both sides (servants, American landlords, Sadao) · self-interest as ironic saviour · courage of Hana · open ending
:::
