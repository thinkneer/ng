# Lost Spring — value points

:::formula Lost Spring — plot in six beats
Saheb scrounging garbage · promise of a school · barefoot "tradition" · Seemapuri: garbage is gold · tea-stall canister, freedom lost · Firozabad: Mukesh, furnaces, Savita, old woman, vicious circle, dream of a garage
:::

:::formula Saheb — value points
Migrant from Dhaka · name means lord of the universe · barefoot ragpicker in Seemapuri · garbage = wonder · tennis shoes with a hole · tea stall: 800 rupees, meals, canister, no longer his own master
:::

:::formula Seemapuri — value points
Edge of Delhi, miles away in condition · mud huts, tin/tarpaulin roofs · no sewage, drainage, water · 10,000 ragpickers from Bangladesh (1971) · ration cards over identity · food decides where tents go
:::

:::formula Mukesh — value points
Son of a bangle maker · wants to be a motor mechanic · will walk to a garage · dares to dream in a town that follows fathers · embarrassed about planes · flash of daring, realistic dream
:::

:::formula Vicious circle — value points
Sahukars · middlemen · policemen · keepers of law · bureaucrats · politicians · fear of jail stops a cooperative · no leader · caste stigma and belief in destiny · baggage passed from father to son
:::

:::formula Bangle industry — value points
Firozabad = glass bangle centre · hot furnaces, dark airless cells · children lose eyesight · 20,000 child workers · law ignored · bangles = suhaag, yet makers know no joy · old woman: bangles, no full meal
:::

:::formula Title and theme — value points
Spring = childhood · stolen by poverty, migration, child labour, tradition, exploitation · both boys work instead of learning · hope survives in wonder (Saheb) and ambition (Mukesh)
:::
