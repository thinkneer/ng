# The Interview — value points

:::formula Part I — the interview in brief
130 years old · commonplace of journalism · truth and art vs intrusion that diminishes · Naipaul, Carroll, Kipling, Wells, Bellow · Kipling–Twain, Wells–Stalin paradox · Denis Brian: interviewer's power · serviceable medium
:::

:::formula Critics of the interview — value points
Naipaul: wounded, lose part of themselves · Carroll: just horror, never consented · Kipling: immoral, crime, assault (Caroline's diary, Boston 1892) · Wells: ordeal (1894) · Bellow: thumbprints on the windpipe
:::

:::formula Case for the interview — value points
Source of truth · an art in practice · supremely serviceable medium · Brian: most vivid impressions of contemporaries come through interviews · one person asking another · interviewer's unprecedented power
:::

:::formula Umberto Eco — profile
University of Bologna · semiotics, literary interpretation, medieval aesthetics · 40+ scholarly works · five novels · children's books, columns · The Name of the Rose 1980 · academic first
:::

:::formula Eco's working method — value points
Interstices = empty time · visitor in the lift · universe shrinks to a fist · same interests across forms · narrative scholarship since dissertation at 22 · novels at fifty by accident · weekend novelist
:::

:::formula The Name of the Rose — value points
Serious detective novel · metaphysics, theology, medieval history · publisher expected 3,000 · sold 10–15 million · a mystery to Eco · timing · readers want difficulty too
:::
