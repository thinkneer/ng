# Invitations and replies — format sheet

:::formula Formal invitation card — parts
Box · host (centred) · "request the pleasure of your company" · occasion · day, date, time · venue · chief guest · RSVP bottom left · third person · no signature
:::

:::formula Informal invitation — parts
Sender's address · date · Dear …, · body ~50 words (occasion, date, time, venue, request to come/confirm) · Your friend, · name · first person
:::

:::formula Formal reply — value points
Thank the host · name occasion and date · accept ("will be happy to attend") or regret ("regret their inability to attend owing to …") · good wishes · third person · boxed
:::

:::formula Informal reply — value points
Thank warmly · name the occasion · clear yes or no · brief reason if no · wish / look forward · first person letter shell
:::

:::formula Invitation — marks split (4)
Format 1 (right shell and person) · Content 2 (host, occasion, date, time, venue; or thanks, occasion, decision, reason) · Expression 1 (tone, grammar, ~50 words)
:::
