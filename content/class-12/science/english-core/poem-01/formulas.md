# My Mother at Sixty-six — value points

:::formula My Mother at Sixty-six — the poem in five moves
Drive to Cochin airport · mother dozing, ashen, corpse-like · poet looks away: sprinting trees, merry children · airport: mother pale as late winter's moon, childhood fear returns · smile, smile, smile — see you soon
:::

:::formula Images of the mother — value points
Dozing, open mouth · ashen face · simile: corpse · later: wan, pale · simile: late winter's moon · age, fading, approaching death
:::

:::formula Images of life — value points
Young trees sprinting (personification) · merry children spilling out of homes · movement, energy, youth · contrast with the mother · poet's attempt to escape pain
:::

:::formula The ending — value points
Old familiar ache = childhood fear of losing mother · says nothing of it · smiles repeatedly · hides fear, comforts mother, hopes to meet again · brave face of love
:::

:::formula Devices — value points
Simile · imagery · contrast/juxtaposition · personification · repetition · symbolism (moon, children, airport) · free verse · single sentence · enjambment · reflective tone
:::

:::formula Themes — value points
Ageing and mortality · fear of separation and loss · love and helplessness · putting on a brave face · cycle of life · acceptance
:::
