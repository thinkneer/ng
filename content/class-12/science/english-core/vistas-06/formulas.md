# Memories of Childhood — value points

:::formula Memories of Childhood — the two parts in brief
Part I Zitkala-Sa: first day at Carlisle, bells, alien clothes, Judewin's warning, hides, dragged, tied, hair shorn, spirit lost · Part II Bama: slow walk home, elder carrying vadai by its string, laughter, Annan explains untouchability, anger, advice to study, first in class
:::

:::formula Zitkala-Sa — value points
Native American (Dakota) · Carlisle Indian School · bitter cold · bells, eating by formula · stiff shoes, tight dresses · Judewin · shingled hair = cowards, mourners · hides under bed · kicks, scratches · tied to chair · braid cut · herded animal · later writer and activist
:::

:::formula Hair and identity — value points
Long hair = dignity and culture · cut only for mourning · shingled = cowardice · forced cutting = forced assimilation · loss of spirit · cultural oppression as "education"
:::

:::formula Bama — value points
Tamil Dalit · Karukku · third standard · street sights: monkey, snake charmer, cyclist, temple, Pongal, stalls, puppets · threshing floor · landlord · elder with packet by string, bows · laughs · Annan explains · anger · studies · first in class · friends
:::

:::formula Annan — value points
Elder brother · explains caste and untouchability · asked which street he lived on · no honour given at birth · study hard, make progress · people will come of their own accord · Bama follows him
:::

:::formula Shared themes — value points
Marginalised communities · childhood discovery of discrimination · loss of dignity · race vs caste · physical resistance vs education · Judewin's submission vs Annan's advice · autobiography as protest · we too are human beings
:::
