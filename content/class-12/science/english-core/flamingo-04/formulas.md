# The Rattrap — value points

:::formula The Rattrap — plot in six beats
Peddler and his rattrap theory · crofter's porridge, cards and thirty kronor · theft, lost in the forest · Ramsjö forge, ironmaster's mistake, refusal · Edla persuades, Christmas Eve, exposure at breakfast, Edla's plea · church news, rattrap gift, letter signed Captain von Stahle
:::

:::formula The peddler — value points
Nameless vagabond · sells wire rattraps, begs, steals · bitter about the world · rattrap theory · steals from a kind host · trapped in forest · transformed by Edla · returns money · signs as captain
:::

:::formula Rattrap metaphor — value points
World = rattrap · baits = riches, joys, food, shelter, heat, clothes · greed closes the trap · peddler takes the bait (thirty kronor) and is trapped (forest) · kindness is the only way out · signature: raised to captain, freed
:::

:::formula The crofter — value points
Old, alone, talkative · porridge, tobacco, mjölis · former Ramsjö worker · cow gives thirty kronor a month · shows the notes in a pouch · trusting to a fault · robbed next morning · money returned through Edla
:::

:::formula The ironmaster — value points
Rich owner of Ramsjö · nightly round of the forge · mistakes stranger for Captain von Stahle · hearty invitation · angry at breakfast, wants sheriff · grumbles about silver · gives in to Edla · fears the worst after church
:::

:::formula Edla Willmansson — value points
Modest, shy, not pretty · compassionate and observant · sees his fear, promises freedom · persuades him to come · pleads for one day of peace · suit as gift, invites him back · receives the rattrap and letter
:::

:::formula Message — value points
Human goodness lies dormant in everyone · compassion and trust awaken it · punishment would not · loneliness is universal · dignity given becomes dignity lived
:::
