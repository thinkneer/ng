# Notice — must know

## Most-repeated board questions

Stars are estimates: ★★★ most years · ★★ often · ★ sometimes. Marks in square brackets; word limit about 50 words (body only).

1. ★★★ Notice announcing a school event (competition, camp, drive, fair) with date, time, venue, eligibility, registration. [4]
2. ★★★ Lost-and-found notice (bag, watch, spectacles, water bottle) with description and whom to contact. [4]
3. ★★ Notice calling a meeting (club, council, residents' association) with agenda. [4]
4. ★★ Notice from a housing society / RWA: water supply cut, cleanliness drive, Diwali mela, parking rules. [4]
5. ★ Notice informing a change: school timings, holiday, new library rules. [4]

## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Notice:** a brief formal public announcement for a group, written in a box.
- **Word limit:** about 50 words, counted for the body only.
- **Order of parts:** issuing body, NOTICE, date, title, body, name, designation.
- **Issuing body:** the school / society / club name, centred at the top, never your own name.
- **Date:** left side, below NOTICE, written in full.
- **Title:** short phrase in capitals or bold stating the subject.
- **Five Ws:** what, when, where, for whom, whom to contact.
- **Voice:** third person or passive; never "I" or "you".
- **Standard contact phrase:** "contact the undersigned".
- **Signature block:** name on one line, designation on the next, at the left.
- **Marks split:** format 1, content 2, expression 1.

## Key formulas

:::formula Notice format
Box → **Issuing body** (centred) → **NOTICE** (centred) → **Date** (left) → **TITLE** (caps) → **Body** (~50 words, five Ws) → **Name** → **Designation**
:::

:::formula Notice opening sentences
Event: *All students are hereby informed that … will be held on … at … in …*<br>
Lost/found: *A … was lost / found in … on … It is / contains …*<br>
Meeting: *A meeting of … will be held on … at … in … to discuss …*<br>
Close: *Interested students may register with the undersigned by …* / *For details, contact the undersigned.*
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton 4-mark notice
1. Draw box; write issuing body and NOTICE centred — format ½<br>
2. Date (full) on the left; TITLE in caps — format ½<br>
3. Body sentence 1: audience + what + when + where — content 1<br>
4. Body sentence 2–3: eligibility / what to bring / registration + deadline + "the undersigned" — content 1<br>
5. Formal third-person tone, correct grammar, ~50 words — expression 1
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- A notice is written inside a box.
- The word NOTICE comes below the issuing body and above the date.
- The title of a notice states its subject in a few words.
- The signature block carries name and designation only, no address.
- A notice uses passive voice or third person throughout.
- "The undersigned" refers to the person signing the notice.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Writing "I" or "you"
First and second person break the formal, impersonal tone and cost the expression mark. Use "students are requested", "interested members may".
:::

:::tip Missing a W
Forgetting the time or the venue is the commonest content deduction. Tick what–when–where–who–contact before boxing the notice.
:::

:::tip Over the limit
A 90-word notice reads as a paragraph, not a notice. Keep the body at 45–55 words; headings do not count.
:::

:::tip Wrong designation
Your designation must match the role in the question exactly; the issuing body must fit that role (club → school; RWA → society).
:::
