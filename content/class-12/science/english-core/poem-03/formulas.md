# A Thing of Beauty — value points

:::formula A Thing of Beauty — the poem in five moves
Beauty is a joy for ever; loveliness increases · gives bower, sleep, health · flowery band binds us to earth despite despondence and gloom; beauty lifts the pall · catalogue: sun, moon, trees, daffodils, rills, musk-rose · mighty dead, lovely tales · endless fountain of immortal drink from heaven
:::

:::formula Permanence of beauty — value points
Joy for ever · loveliness increases · never passes into nothingness · kept and grown in memory · endless fountain · heavenly source · immortal drink
:::

:::formula Beauty's gifts — value points
Quiet bower (shelter) · sleep full of sweet dreams · health · quiet breathing · flowery band binding us to life · removes the pall from dark spirits
:::

:::formula Human suffering — value points
Despondence · inhuman dearth of noble natures · gloomy days · unhealthy, over-darkened ways · pall over the spirit · beauty as the daily remedy
:::

:::formula Catalogue — value points
Sun and moon · old and young trees, shady boon for sheep · daffodils, green world · clear rills, cooling covert, hot season · mid-forest brake, musk-rose blooms · grandeur of dooms of the mighty dead · lovely tales heard or read
:::

:::formula Devices and form — value points
Metaphor: bower, flowery band, pall, fountain · imagery · alliteration · antithesis · inversion · rhymed (heroic) couplets · iambic pentameter · enjambment · consoling tone
:::
