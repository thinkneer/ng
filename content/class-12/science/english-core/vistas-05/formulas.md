# On the Face of It — value points

:::formula On the Face of It — the play in six beats
Derry climbs into the garden; Mr Lamb welcomes him · Derry's bitterness about his face · Mr Lamb's images: bees sing, no weeds, everyone has something, the frightened man, hatred burns · Derry promises to return · Scene 2: mother forbids, Derry runs back · Mr Lamb falls from the ladder; Derry weeps
:::

:::formula Derry — value points
Fourteen · acid-burnt face · climbs the wall · expects rejection · stares, whispers, mother's kiss on the other side · hates pity, hates Beauty and the Beast · wants to be alone · changes: questions, promise, defies mother, returns, weeps
:::

:::formula Mr Lamb — value points
Old · tin leg from the war · Lamey-Lamb · open gate · bees, crab apples, ladder · no weeds · watch, listen, think · stories not sermons · lonely, no family, taunted · cheerful by choice · falls picking apples
:::

:::formula Mr Lamb's lessons — value points
Open gate = openness to people · bees sing = attitude decides · growing things = labels are choices · everyone has something · the man killed by a picture = hiding is no safety · hatred burns worse than acid · use what you have
:::

:::formula Scene 2 and the ending — value points
Mother forbids on hearsay · Derry: face not important, wants people and things · runs three miles back · ladder slips · Mr Lamb falls · Derry kneels, weeps · tragedy and hope · open ending
:::

:::formula Themes and craft — value points
Appearance vs reality (title pun) · disability and society · isolation vs engagement · hatred as the real wound · loneliness of the kind · change through acceptance · radio play, two scenes, dialogue-driven, symbolic garden
:::
