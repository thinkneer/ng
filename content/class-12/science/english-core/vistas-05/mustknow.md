## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Author:** Susan Hill, English novelist and playwright; the piece is a radio play in two scenes.
- **Derry:** fourteen; one side of his face burnt by acid; bitter, withdrawn.
- **Mr Lamb:** old man with a tin leg (lost in the war); children call him Lamey-Lamb.
- **Setting:** Mr Lamb's garden — gate always open, bees, crab-apple trees, a ladder.
- **How Derry enters:** climbs over the wall, thinking the garden empty.
- **Derry's complaints:** people stare and look away; whispers on the bus; mother kisses the other side of his face; hates pity and comparisons.
- **Beauty and the Beast:** Derry hates it — no one will ever kiss his face and turn him into a prince.
- **Derry's wish at first:** to be alone, away from people.
- **Bees:** Derry hears buzzing; Mr Lamb hears singing — same world, different attitude.
- **Weeds and flowers:** Mr Lamb calls all plants growing things; labels are choices.
- **The frightened man:** locked himself in his room; a picture fell on his head and killed him.
- **Lesson of the story:** hiding from life does not make you safe, only alone.
- **Everyone has something:** burnt face, tin leg, fear of the dark — all the same inside.
- **Mr Lamb's advice:** watch, listen, think; use your arms, legs, eyes, ears, brain.
- **On hatred:** hating burns worse than acid.
- **Mr Lamb's loneliness:** no family; people come and go; children taunt him; cheerfulness is a choice.
- **Derry's promise:** to come back and help with the apples after telling his mother.
- **Mother's ban:** forbids him to return on hearsay about the old man.
- **Derry's reply:** his face is not important; he wants to be with people and do things; runs back.
- **Ending:** Mr Lamb falls from the ladder picking apples; Derry kneels beside him weeping.
- **Title:** pun on judging by appearances.
- **Themes:** appearance vs reality, disability and society, isolation vs engagement, hatred, loneliness, change.

## Key formulas

:::formula Mr Lamb's philosophy — value points
Open gate, anyone welcome · bees sing, not buzz · no weeds, only growing things · everyone has something · watch, listen, think · the frightened man died anyway · hatred burns worse than acid · live, don't hide.
:::

:::formula Derry's bitterness — value points
Acid-burnt face · stares and whispers · "only a mother could love" · mother kisses the other side · hates pity · hates Beauty and the Beast · wants to be alone · expects nothing.
:::

:::formula Derry's change — value points
Listens to the bees · asks questions · promises to return for apples · tells mother his face is not important · wants to be with people · runs back · kneels beside the fallen Mr Lamb — cares.
:::

:::formula The ending — value points
Ladder slips, Mr Lamb falls · Derry arrives too late · weeps · tragedy: the one who accepted him is lost · hope: Derry came back · irony: the welcoming man falls alone · open ending.
:::

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Not a story, a play
Refer to scenes and dialogue; the characters speak, there is no narrator.
:::

:::tip Mr Lamb is lonely too
His cheerfulness is chosen; he has no family and children taunt him. Mention this for the full picture.
:::

:::tip The frightened man's story
Its point is that hiding from life brings no safety — not that accidents are unavoidable.
:::

:::tip Derry's return is the change
Do not end an answer on the fall alone; Derry chose people over hiding.
:::
