# Ch 12 · Atoms — formulas

## Alpha-particle scattering

- Gold foil: most α particles pass undeviated (atom mostly empty); a few deflected beyond 90° (tiny, dense, positive nucleus).

:::formula Closest approach
$r_0=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{2Ze^2}{K}$;   small impact parameter $b$ → large deflection θ
:::

## Bohr model of hydrogen

:::formula Bohr model
$mvr=\dfrac{nh}{2\pi}$;   $h\nu=E_i-E_f$<br>
$r_n=\dfrac{n^2h^2\varepsilon_0}{\pi me^2}=0.529\,n^2$ Å;   $v_n=\dfrac{e^2}{2\varepsilon_0nh}\approx\dfrac{2.18\times10^6}{n}$ m s⁻¹<br>
$E_n=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV;   $K=-E$, $U=2E$<br>
$\dfrac1\lambda=R\left(\dfrac1{n_f^2}-\dfrac1{n_i^2}\right)$, $R=1.097\times10^7$ m⁻¹
:::

| Series | $n_f$ | $n_i$ | Region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lyman | 1 | 2, 3, … | UV (longest 121.6 nm; limit 91.2 nm) |
| Balmer | 2 | 3, 4, … | visible (H$_\alpha$ 656.3 nm; limit 364.6 nm) |
| Paschen / Brackett / Pfund | 3 / 4 / 5 | $n_f+1,\dots$ | infrared |

- Hydrogen: ionisation energy 13.6 eV; first excitation 10.2 eV; second 12.09 eV. De Broglie: $2\pi r_n=n\lambda$.
- Bohr model limits: only H-like atoms; no line intensities.
