## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Alpha-scattering result 1:** most α particles pass undeflected, so the atom is mostly empty.
- **Alpha-scattering result 2:** a few bounce back, so positive charge and mass sit in a tiny nucleus.
- **Size of nucleus:** about $10^{-15}$–$10^{-14}$ m; atom about $10^{-10}$ m.
- **Impact parameter:** perpendicular distance of the α particle's initial path from the nucleus.
- **Distance of closest approach:** separation at which a head-on α particle momentarily stops.
- **Rutherford model failure:** orbiting electron should radiate, spiral in and give a continuous spectrum.
- **Bohr's first postulate:** electrons revolve in certain stationary orbits without radiating.
- **Bohr's second postulate:** angular momentum $mvr=nh/2\pi$.
- **Bohr's third postulate:** photon emitted on a jump, $h\nu=E_i-E_f$.
- **Ground state:** lowest energy state, n = 1.
- **Ionisation energy of hydrogen:** 13.6 eV.
- **First excitation energy of hydrogen:** 10.2 eV (n = 1 to 2).
- **De Broglie and Bohr:** stable orbit holds a whole number of wavelengths, $2\pi r=n\lambda$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Distance of closest approach
$r_0=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{2Ze^2}{K}$.
:::

:::formula Bohr radius of the nth orbit
$r_n=\dfrac{n^2h^2\varepsilon_0}{\pi me^2}=0.529\,n^2$ Å.
:::

:::formula Speed in the nth orbit
$v_n=\dfrac{e^2}{2\varepsilon_0nh}$, $v_n\propto\dfrac1n$.
:::

:::formula Energy of the nth orbit
$E_n=-\dfrac{me^4}{8\varepsilon_0^2n^2h^2}=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV; $K=-E$, $U=2E$.
:::

:::formula Rydberg formula
$\dfrac1\lambda=R\left(\dfrac1{n_f^2}-\dfrac1{n_i^2}\right)$, $R=1.097\times10^7$ m⁻¹.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Dv · Bohr radius and energy (5 marks)
$mv^2/r=ke^2/r^2$ [½] + $mvr=nh/2\pi$ [½] → $r_n=n^2h^2\varepsilon_0/\pi me^2$ [1½] → $K=e^2/8\pi\varepsilon_0r$, $U=-e^2/4\pi\varepsilon_0r$ [1] → $E_n=-me^4/8\varepsilon_0^2n^2h^2=-13.6/n^2$ eV [1½]
:::

:::skeleton N · Spectral line (2 marks)
$\Delta E=E_{n_i}-E_{n_f}$ in eV [½] → $\lambda=1240/\Delta E$ nm (or Rydberg formula) [1] → name the series and region [½]
:::

:::skeleton Df · Alpha scattering (3 marks)
Set-up: α source, gold foil, ZnS screen [½] → three observations (most through; few large angles; ~1 in 8000 beyond 90°) [1] → conclusions: empty space; tiny massive positive nucleus; electrons outside [1½]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Ratio of radii $r_1:r_2:r_3$:** 1 : 4 : 9.
- **Speed as n increases:** decreases ($\propto1/n$).
- **Energy as n increases:** increases towards zero.
- **Lyman series region:** ultraviolet.
- **Balmer series region:** visible.
- **Paschen, Brackett, Pfund:** infrared.
- **Longest wavelength of a series:** smallest jump, $n_i=n_f+1$.
- **Series limit:** $n_i=\infty$, shortest wavelength.
- **Head-on α particle:** deflection 180°, impact parameter zero.
- **Bohr model valid for:** hydrogen and one-electron ions only.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sign of energy
$E_n$ is negative; a jump to higher n absorbs energy, a jump down emits it.
:::

:::tip K and U in an orbit
$K=+13.6/n^2$ eV and $U=-27.2/n^2$ eV for hydrogen; total $E=-13.6/n^2$ eV.
:::

:::tip Which n is final
In $1/\lambda=R(1/n_f^2-1/n_i^2)$ the lower level is $n_f$; swapping gives a negative λ.
:::

:::tip Thomson cannot bounce
Only a concentrated nucleus can turn an α particle back; say this explicitly in the conclusion.
:::
