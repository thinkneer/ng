# Ch 1 · Electric Charges and Fields — formulas

## Charge and Coulomb's law

- Quantisation $q=\pm ne$, $e=1.6\times10^{-19}$ C; charge is additive (scalar) and conserved.

:::formula Coulomb's law
$F=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$;   $\vec F_{21}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r_{21}^2}\hat r_{21}$<br>
$\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}=9\times10^9$ N m² C⁻²;   $\varepsilon_0=8.854\times10^{-12}$ C² N⁻¹ m⁻²<br>
In a medium: $F_m=F/K$, $K=\varepsilon_r=\varepsilon/\varepsilon_0$
:::

- Superposition: $\vec F=\sum\vec F_i$, $\vec E=\sum\vec E_i$ (add as vectors).

## Electric field

:::formula Electric field
$\vec E=\vec F/q_0$ (test charge $q_0\to0$);   $\vec F=q\vec E$<br>
Point charge: $E=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q}{r^2}$ (N C⁻¹ = V m⁻¹)
:::

- Field lines: + to −; never cross; no closed loops; denser where $E$ is larger.

## Electric dipole

:::formula Dipole field and torque
$\vec p=q\cdot2\vec a$ (from −q to +q, C m)<br>
Axial: $E=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{2pr}{(r^2-a^2)^2}\approx\dfrac{2kp}{r^3}$ (along $\vec p$)<br>
Equatorial: $E=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{p}{(r^2+a^2)^{3/2}}\approx\dfrac{kp}{r^3}$ (opposite $\vec p$)<br>
Uniform field: $\vec\tau=\vec p\times\vec E$, $\tau=pE\sin\theta$; net force $=0$
:::

- Non-uniform field: torque **and** net force. Stable equilibrium at $\theta=0^\circ$, unstable at $180^\circ$.

## Flux and Gauss's law

:::formula Flux and Gauss's law
$\phi=\vec E\cdot\vec S=ES\cos\theta$ (N m² C⁻¹);   $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec S=\dfrac{q_\text{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}$<br>
Densities: $\lambda=dq/dl$, $\sigma=dq/dA$, $\rho=dq/dV$
:::

:::formula Gauss's law results
Long wire: $E=\dfrac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0r}$<br>
Infinite sheet: $E=\dfrac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$ (independent of $r$); two opposite sheets: $\sigma/\varepsilon_0$ between, 0 outside<br>
Thin shell: $E=\dfrac{kq}{r^2}$ ($r\ge R$), $E=0$ ($r<R$)
:::

- Charge at cube centre: $q/\varepsilon_0$ total, $q/6\varepsilon_0$ per face. Charge at a cube corner: $q/8\varepsilon_0$ through the cube, $q/24\varepsilon_0$ through each of the 3 far faces.
