# Ch 6 · Electromagnetic Induction — formulas

## Faraday and Lenz

:::formula Flux and induced emf
$\Phi_B=\vec B\cdot\vec A=BA\cos\theta$ (Wb = T m²);   $\varepsilon=-N\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$<br>
Induced charge $q=\dfrac{N\,\Delta\Phi}{R}$ (independent of time)
:::

- Lenz's law: the induced current opposes the **change** in flux; a consequence of energy conservation.
- Magnet falling through a coil: $a<g$. Loop moving in a uniform field with no change in flux: no emf.

## Motional emf

:::formula Moving rod
$\varepsilon=Blv$;   $I=\dfrac{Blv}{R}$;   retarding force $F=\dfrac{B^2l^2v}{R}$;   power $=\dfrac{B^2l^2v^2}{R}=I^2R$
:::

## Inductance

:::formula Self and mutual inductance
Self: $N\Phi=LI$, $\varepsilon=-L\dfrac{dI}{dt}$;   solenoid $L=\mu_0n^2Al=\dfrac{\mu_0N^2A}{l}$ (× $\mu_r$ with a core)<br>
Mutual: $N_2\Phi_2=MI_1$, $\varepsilon_2=-M\dfrac{dI_1}{dt}$;   coaxial solenoids $M=\mu_0n_1n_2\pi r_1^2l$;   $M_{12}=M_{21}$<br>
Energy $U=\dfrac12LI^2$;   magnetic energy density $u_B=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}$
:::

- Inductance (henry) is electrical inertia: it opposes changes in current.
- Not in boards: eddy currents. AC generator is in Ch 7.
