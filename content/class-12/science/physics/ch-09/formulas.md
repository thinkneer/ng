# Ch 9 · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments — formulas

## Mirrors (Cartesian sign convention, from the pole)

:::formula Mirror formula
$f=\dfrac R2$;   $\dfrac1v+\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f$;   $m=\dfrac{h'}{h}=-\dfrac vu$
:::

## Refraction and TIR

:::formula Refraction
Snell: $n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r$;   $n_{21}=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}$;   $\lambda_m=\dfrac\lambda n$, $\nu$ unchanged<br>
Apparent depth $=\dfrac{\text{real depth}}{n}$;   TIR: $\sin c=\dfrac1{n_{21}}$ (denser → rarer, $i>c$)<br>
Spherical surface: $\dfrac{n_2}{v}-\dfrac{n_1}{u}=\dfrac{n_2-n_1}{R}$
:::

- TIR uses: mirage, sparkle of diamond ($c\approx24.4^\circ$), optical fibres, totally reflecting prisms (glass $c\approx42^\circ$).

## Lenses

:::formula Lenses
Lens maker: $\dfrac1f=(n_{21}-1)\left(\dfrac1{R_1}-\dfrac1{R_2}\right)$;   lens: $\dfrac1v-\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f$;   $m=\dfrac vu$<br>
Power $P=\dfrac1{f(\text{m})}$ dioptre;   contact: $\dfrac1F=\sum\dfrac1{f_i}$, $P=\sum P_i$, $m=m_1m_2$
:::

## Prism

:::formula Prism
$r_1+r_2=A$;   $i+e=A+\delta$;   minimum deviation: $n=\dfrac{\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}{2}}{\sin\frac A2}$ ($i=e$, $r=\frac A2$)<br>
Thin prism: $\delta=(n-1)A$
:::

- Dispersion: $n_\text{violet}>n_\text{red}$, so violet deviates most.

## Optical instruments

:::formula Microscopes and telescopes
Simple microscope: $m=1+\dfrac Df$ (image at $D$), $m=\dfrac Df$ (at ∞); $D=25$ cm<br>
Compound microscope: $m=m_om_e$; normal adj. $m=\dfrac{L}{f_o}\dfrac{D}{f_e}$; near point $m=\dfrac{L}{f_o}\left(1+\dfrac D{f_e}\right)$<br>
Telescope: normal adj. $m=\dfrac{f_o}{f_e}$, length $f_o+f_e$; near point $m=\dfrac{f_o}{f_e}\left(1+\dfrac{f_e}{D}\right)$
:::

- Microscope: both $f$ short, $f_o<f_e$. Telescope: $f_o$ large, $f_e$ small, large aperture.
- Reflecting (Cassegrain) telescope: no chromatic aberration, paraboloidal mirror removes spherical aberration, lighter and easier to support.
