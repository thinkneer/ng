## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Cartesian sign convention:** distances from the pole; along incident light positive, against it negative.
- **Focal length of a spherical mirror:** half its radius of curvature, $f=R/2$.
- **Refractive index:** $n=c/v$; also $n_{21}=\sin i/\sin r$.
- **On refraction:** frequency stays the same; wavelength and speed change.
- **Critical angle:** angle of incidence in the denser medium for which the angle of refraction is 90°.
- **Conditions for TIR:** denser to rarer medium, and angle of incidence greater than critical angle.
- **Optical fibre:** guides light by repeated TIR in a core of higher index than its cladding.
- **Power of a lens:** reciprocal of focal length in metres; unit dioptre.
- **Angle of minimum deviation:** smallest deviation; then $i=e$ and the ray inside is symmetric.
- **Dispersion:** splitting of white light into colours because $n$ depends on wavelength.
- **Near point (D):** least distance of distinct vision, 25 cm.
- **Magnifying power:** ratio of angle subtended by the image to that by the object.
- **Normal adjustment:** final image at infinity.
- **Reflecting telescope:** uses a concave mirror as objective; no chromatic aberration.

## Key formulas

:::formula Mirror formula
$\dfrac1v+\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f$; $m=-\dfrac vu$; $f=\dfrac R2$.
:::

:::formula Snell's law
$n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r$; $n_{21}=v_1/v_2$.
:::

:::formula Critical angle
$\sin c=\dfrac{1}{n_{21}}$ (denser to rarer).
:::

:::formula Refraction at a spherical surface
$\dfrac{n_2}{v}-\dfrac{n_1}{u}=\dfrac{n_2-n_1}{R}$.
:::

:::formula Lens maker's formula
$\dfrac1f=(n_{21}-1)\left(\dfrac1{R_1}-\dfrac1{R_2}\right)$.
:::

:::formula Thin lens formula
$\dfrac1v-\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f$; $m=\dfrac vu$.
:::

:::formula Power of lenses in contact
$P=\dfrac1{f(\text{m})}$; $P=P_1+P_2$; $\dfrac1F=\dfrac1{f_1}+\dfrac1{f_2}$.
:::

:::formula Prism at minimum deviation
$n=\dfrac{\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}{2}}{\sin\frac A2}$; $r=A/2$; thin prism $\delta=(n-1)A$.
:::

:::formula Compound microscope magnification
Normal adjustment $m=\dfrac{L}{f_o}\dfrac{D}{f_e}$; near point $m=\dfrac{L}{f_o}\left(1+\dfrac D{f_e}\right)$.
:::

:::formula Telescope magnification
Normal adjustment $m=\dfrac{f_o}{f_e}$, $L=f_o+f_e$; near point $m=\dfrac{f_o}{f_e}\left(1+\dfrac{f_e}{D}\right)$.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Dv · Lens maker (3 marks)
Surface 1: $\frac{n_2}{v_1}-\frac{n_1}{u}=\frac{n_2-n_1}{R_1}$ [1] → surface 2 with $I_1$ as object: $\frac{n_1}{v}-\frac{n_2}{v_1}=\frac{n_1-n_2}{R_2}$ [½] → add [½] → $u=\infty$, $v=f$ ⇒ lens maker's; general ⇒ $\frac1v-\frac1u=\frac1f$ [1]
:::

:::skeleton G · Compound microscope (3 marks)
Ray diagram: objective forms real inverted image inside $f_e$ (or at $F_e$); eyepiece forms final virtual image; arrows on rays [1½] → $m=m_om_e$, $m_o=L/f_o$, $m_e=D/f_e$ or $1+D/f_e$ [1½]
:::

:::skeleton G · Astronomical telescope (3 marks)
Diagram: parallel rays at angle α, image at common focus, emergent parallel rays at β [1½] → $m=\beta/\alpha=f_o/f_e$, $L=f_o+f_e$ [1½]
:::

:::skeleton Dv · Prism (3 marks)
Diagram with normals, $i$, $e$, $r_1$, $r_2$ [½] → $r_1+r_2=A$ [½] → $\delta=(i-r_1)+(e-r_2)=i+e-A$ [1] → at $\delta_m$: $i=e$, $r=A/2$ ⇒ $n=\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}2/\sin\frac A2$ [1]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Concave mirror / concave lens f:** negative; convex mirror / convex lens f: positive.
- **Real image sign:** mirror $v<0$; lens $v>0$.
- **Plane mirror:** $m=+1$; image as far behind as the object is in front.
- **Lens in a liquid of the same n:** behaves like a plane sheet ($f\to\infty$).
- **Violet vs red in glass:** violet has larger n, deviates more, travels slower.
- **Critical angle of glass / diamond:** about 42° / 24.4°.
- **Totally reflecting prism:** uses 45° > 42° critical angle.
- **Microscope objective:** short focal length; telescope objective: long focal length, large aperture.
- **Telescope final image:** inverted.
- **Large aperture of objective:** more light and better resolving power.
- **δ–i graph of a prism:** U-shaped with a minimum at $\delta_m$.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sign convention
Put signs on u, f and R before substituting; never on the unknown.
:::

:::tip Lens and mirror magnification differ
Mirror $m=-v/u$; lens $m=+v/u$.
:::

:::tip Power needs metres
$P=1/f$ with $f$ in metres: $f=20$ cm gives 5 D, not 0.05 D.
:::

:::tip n relative to the surroundings
In the lens maker's formula use $n_\text{lens}/n_\text{medium}$.
:::

:::tip Arrows on ray diagrams
Missing arrows or a wrongly placed image loses the diagram mark; label objective, eyepiece, $F_o$, $F_e$.
:::
