## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Work function:** minimum energy needed to remove an electron from a metal surface.
- **Photoelectric effect:** emission of electrons from a metal when light of suitable frequency falls on it.
- **Threshold frequency:** minimum frequency for photoelectric emission from a metal.
- **Stopping potential:** negative collector potential at which photocurrent becomes zero.
- **Saturation current:** maximum photocurrent, when all emitted electrons are collected.
- **Photocurrent depends on:** intensity of light (for $\nu>\nu_0$).
- **Maximum kinetic energy depends on:** frequency, not intensity.
- **Photoelectric emission:** instantaneous (≈$10^{-9}$ s).
- **Photon, quantum of light:** quantum of light energy $h\nu$, momentum $h/\lambda$, zero rest mass, no charge.
- **Intensity of light (photon picture):** number of photons crossing unit area per second.
- **Slope of the $V_0$–$\nu$ graph:** $h/e$, the same for all metals.
- **de Broglie hypothesis:** moving matter has an associated wave of wavelength $h/p$.
- **Matter waves of everyday objects:** wavelength too small to detect.

## Key formulas

:::formula Einstein's photoelectric equation
$K_\text{max}=h\nu-\phi_0=eV_0$.
:::

:::formula Work function and threshold
$\phi_0=h\nu_0=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_0}$.
:::

:::formula Stopping potential against frequency
$V_0=\dfrac he\nu-\dfrac{\phi_0}{e}$; slope $h/e$, intercept $\nu_0$.
:::

:::formula Photon energy and momentum
$E=h\nu=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$, $E(\text{eV})=\dfrac{1240}{\lambda(\text{nm})}$; $p=\dfrac h\lambda$.
:::

:::formula Photons per second
$n=\dfrac{P}{h\nu}=\dfrac{P\lambda}{hc}$.
:::

:::formula de Broglie wavelength
$\lambda=\dfrac hp=\dfrac{h}{mv}=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2mK}}=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2mqV}}$.
:::

:::formula Electron accelerated through V volts
$\lambda=\dfrac{1.227}{\sqrt V}$ nm.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Light below threshold, very intense:** no emission.
- **Intensity doubled (ν fixed):** photocurrent doubles, $V_0$ unchanged.
- **Frequency increased (intensity fixed):** $V_0$ increases.
- **Metals for visible light:** alkali metals (Cs, K, Na) with low work function.
- **Photons in electric or magnetic fields:** not deflected.
- **Same voltage:** $\lambda_e>\lambda_p>\lambda_\alpha$.
- **Same momentum:** equal de Broglie wavelengths.
- **Doubling the speed of a particle:** de Broglie wavelength halves.
- **Electron through 100 V:** $\lambda\approx0.123$ nm.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip eV versus joule
Use $hc=1240$ eV nm for eV answers, or convert: 1 eV $=1.6\times10^{-19}$ J.
:::

:::tip Stopping potential in volts
$V_0$ in volts equals $K_\text{max}$ in eV; don't write "1.1 eV" for a potential.
:::

:::tip Same K versus same V
For the same kinetic energy $\lambda\propto1/\sqrt m$; for the same voltage $\lambda\propto1/\sqrt{mq}$.
:::

:::tip Davisson–Germer is out
The Davisson–Germer experiment is not in the 2026–27 syllabus.
:::
