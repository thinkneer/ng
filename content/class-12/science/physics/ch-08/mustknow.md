## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Displacement current:** current due to a changing electric flux, $i_d=\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$.
- **Need for displacement current:** makes current continuous through the gap of a charging capacitor.
- **Ampère–Maxwell law:** $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0(i_c+i_d)$.
- **Source of EM waves:** accelerated (oscillating) charges.
- **EM waves are transverse:** $\vec E\perp\vec B\perp$ direction of propagation.
- **Direction of travel:** along $\vec E\times\vec B$.
- **Phase of E and B:** in phase; they peak together.
- **Medium:** EM waves need none; they travel through vacuum.
- **Speed in vacuum:** $c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}=3\times10^8$ m s⁻¹, same for all wavelengths.
- **Energy and momentum:** EM waves carry both and exert radiation pressure.
- **Microwaves:** used in radar and ovens; produced by klystron, magnetron, Gunn diode.
- **Infrared:** produced by hot bodies; used in remote controls and physiotherapy.
- **Ultraviolet:** mostly absorbed by ozone; used for water purification and LASIK.
- **X-rays:** produced when fast electrons hit a metal target; used in diagnosis.
- **Gamma rays:** emitted by nuclei; used in cancer treatment.

## Key formulas

:::formula Displacement current
$i_d=\varepsilon_0\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$; in a charging capacitor $i_d=i_c$.
:::

:::formula Speed of light from μ₀ and ε₀
$c=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}$; in a medium $v=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}}$.
:::

:::formula Ratio of field amplitudes
$\dfrac{E_0}{B_0}=c$.
:::

:::formula Wave relations
$c=\nu\lambda$; $k=2\pi/\lambda$; $\omega=2\pi\nu=ck$.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Increasing frequency order:** radio, micro, IR, visible, UV, X, γ.
- **Shortest wavelength:** gamma rays; longest: radio waves.
- **Waves used in radar:** microwaves.
- **Waves in a TV remote:** infrared.
- **Waves that cause the greenhouse effect:** infrared.
- **Waves absorbed by the ozone layer:** ultraviolet.
- **Waves used to sterilise and purify water:** ultraviolet.
- **Waves for broken-bone imaging:** X-rays.
- **Frequency of a wave from a charge oscillating at ν:** ν.
- **Charge moving at constant velocity:** does not radiate.
- **Speed of visible light vs X-rays in vacuum:** the same, $c$.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Phase versus direction
E and B are perpendicular in direction but in phase in time.
:::

:::tip Displacement current is not charge flow
No charge crosses the gap; the changing electric flux produces the magnetic field.
:::

:::tip Wavelength and frequency run opposite
Highest frequency (γ) means shortest wavelength.
:::

:::tip Units in E₀ = cB₀
$B_0$ in tesla, $c$ in m s⁻¹, so $E_0$ comes out in V m⁻¹ (= N C⁻¹).
:::
