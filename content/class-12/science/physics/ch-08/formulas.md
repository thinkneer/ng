# Ch 8 · Electromagnetic Waves — formulas

## Displacement current and EM waves

:::formula EM waves
Displacement current $i_d=\varepsilon_0\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$;   $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0(i_c+i_d)$<br>
$c=\dfrac1{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}$;   in a medium $v=\dfrac1{\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}}$;   $\dfrac{E_0}{B_0}=c$;   $c=\nu\lambda$<br>
$E_y=E_0\sin(kx-\omega t)$, $B_z=B_0\sin(kx-\omega t)$;   $k=\dfrac{2\pi}\lambda$, $\omega=ck$
:::

- Transverse; $\vec E\perp\vec B\perp$ direction of propagation ($\vec E\times\vec B$); $E$ and $B$ in phase; no medium needed.
- Produced by **accelerated** charges; carry energy and momentum.

## The EM spectrum

| Wave | $\lambda$ range | Source / use |
|---|---|---|
| Radio | > 0.1 m | aerials / AM, FM, TV, mobile |
| Microwave | 0.1 m – 1 mm | klystron, magnetron, Gunn diode / radar, ovens |
| Infrared | 1 mm – 700 nm | hot bodies / remote control, physiotherapy, greenhouse effect |
| Visible | 700 – 400 nm | excited atoms / vision, photography |
| UV | 400 nm – 1 nm | Sun, arcs / LASIK, water purification; ozone absorbs |
| X-rays | 1 nm – $10^{-3}$ nm | X-ray tube / diagnosis, cancer therapy |
| Gamma | < $10^{-3}$ nm | nuclei / cancer treatment, nuclear reactions |

- Increasing frequency: R – M – I – V – U – X – G. All travel at $c$ in vacuum.
