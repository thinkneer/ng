# Ch 5 · Magnetism and Matter — formulas

## Bar magnet as a dipole

:::formula Bar magnet
Bar magnet ≡ solenoid; $\vec m$ from S to N (A m²)<br>
Axial $B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{2m}{r^3}$;   equatorial $B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{m}{r^3}$ (opposite $\vec m$)<br>
$\vec\tau=\vec m\times\vec B$;   $U=-\vec m\cdot\vec B$;   Gauss (magnetism): $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec S=0$
:::

- Field lines are closed loops (no monopoles): outside N → S, inside S → N.

## Magnetic materials

:::formula Magnetisation
$\vec B=\mu_0(\vec H+\vec M)$;   $\vec M=\chi\vec H$;   $\mu_r=1+\chi$;   $\mu=\mu_0\mu_r$<br>
Curie's law (para): $\chi=\dfrac{C\mu_0}{T}$;   ferro above $T_c$: $\chi\propto\dfrac1{T-T_c}$
:::

| | Dia | Para | Ferro |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\chi$ | small −ve | small +ve | ≫ 1 |
| $\mu_r$ | < 1 | > 1 | ≫ 1 |
| examples | Bi, Cu, Pb, H₂O, N₂ | Al, Na, Ca, O₂, CuCl₂ | Fe, Co, Ni, Gd |

- Superconductor: $\chi=-1$ (Meissner effect). Not in boards: Earth's magnetism, hysteresis.
