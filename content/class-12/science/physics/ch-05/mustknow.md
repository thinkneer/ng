## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Magnetic field lines:** closed loops; N to S outside, S to N inside.
- **Gauss's law for magnetism:** net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero.
- **Magnetic monopole:** isolated N or S pole; not found in nature.
- **Bar magnet:** behaves like a solenoid carrying current; moment points S to N.
- **Magnetisation M:** net magnetic moment per unit volume.
- **Magnetic intensity H:** $H=B_0/\mu_0$; inside a solenoid $H=nI$.
- **Magnetic susceptibility:** $\chi=M/H$; dimensionless.
- **Relative permeability:** $\mu_r=\mu/\mu_0=1+\chi$.
- **Diamagnetic:** $\chi$ small negative; weakly repelled; e.g. Bi, Cu, Pb, water, N₂.
- **Paramagnetic:** $\chi$ small positive; weakly attracted; e.g. Al, Na, Ca, O₂, CuCl₂.
- **Ferromagnetic:** $\chi$ very large; strongly attracted; e.g. Fe, Co, Ni, Gd.
- **Domain:** region in a ferromagnet where all atomic moments are aligned.
- **Curie temperature:** above it a ferromagnet becomes paramagnetic.
- **Curie's law:** paramagnetic $\chi=C\mu_0/T$.
- **Meissner effect:** superconductor expels the magnetic field; perfect diamagnet, $\chi=-1$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Field of a short bar magnet
Axial $B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{2m}{r^3}$; equatorial $B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{m}{r^3}$ (opposite $\vec m$).
:::

:::formula Torque and energy of a magnetic dipole
$\vec\tau=\vec m\times\vec B$; $U=-\vec m\cdot\vec B=-mB\cos\theta$.
:::

:::formula Magnetisation and permeability
$\vec B=\mu_0(\vec H+\vec M)$; $\vec M=\chi\vec H$; $\mu_r=1+\chi$; $\mu=\mu_0\mu_r$.
:::

:::formula Curie's law
Paramagnet: $\chi=\dfrac{C\mu_0}{T}$; ferromagnet above $T_c$: $\chi\propto\dfrac{1}{T-T_c}$.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Cutting a bar magnet:** gives two complete magnets, each with N and S.
- **Dia liquid in a U-tube in a field:** level falls in the arm in the field.
- **Dia rod between poles:** sets perpendicular to the field.
- **Para rod between poles:** sets along the field.
- **Temperature and diamagnetism:** practically independent.
- **Superconductor's χ:** −1.
- **Unit of magnetic moment:** A m² (= J T⁻¹).
- **Unit of M and H:** A m⁻¹.
- **Susceptibility and permeability:** dimensionless χ; $\mu_r$ dimensionless.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sign of χ
Dia: negative; para: small positive; ferro: large positive. Don't call diamagnets "non-magnetic".
:::

:::tip Direction of the bar magnet's moment
$\vec m$ points from S to N inside the magnet.
:::

:::tip Out of syllabus
Earth's magnetism and hysteresis are not in boards; don't spend time on them.
:::

:::tip Dipole torque angle
$\theta$ is the angle between $\vec m$ and $\vec B$; torque is maximum at 90°.
:::
