# Ch 10 · Wave Optics — formulas

## Wavefronts and Huygens

- Wavefront: locus of points in the same phase; rays ⊥ wavefronts. Point source → spherical; distant source → plane.
- Huygens: every point is a source of secondary wavelets; new wavefront = forward envelope.

:::formula Snell's law from wavefronts
$\dfrac{\sin i}{\sin r}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=n_{21}$;   frequency unchanged, $\lambda_2=\lambda_1\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$
:::

## Interference and YDSE

:::formula Interference and YDSE
$\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta x$;   $I=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\phi$;   equal: $I=4I_0\cos^2\dfrac\phi2$<br>
Bright: $\Delta x=n\lambda$;   dark: $\Delta x=(2n+1)\dfrac\lambda2$<br>
YDSE: $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$;   bright $y_n=\dfrac{n\lambda D}{d}$;   dark $y_n=\dfrac{(2n+1)\lambda D}{2d}$;   angular width $\dfrac\lambda d$
:::

- Coherent sources: constant phase difference (derived from one source). YDSE in a medium: $\beta'=\beta/n$. White light: central fringe white.

## Single-slit diffraction

:::formula Single slit
Minima $a\sin\theta=n\lambda$ ($n=\pm1,\pm2,\dots$);   central max angular width $\dfrac{2\lambda}{a}$, linear $\dfrac{2\lambda D}{a}$
:::

- Interference: equal fringe widths and intensities. Diffraction: central max twice as wide; intensity falls for higher orders.
- Board: YDSE fringe width as final expression only; single-slit central maximum qualitative. Not in boards: polarisation.
