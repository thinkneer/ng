## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Wavefront:** locus of points vibrating in the same phase.
- **Ray and wavefront:** rays are perpendicular to wavefronts.
- **Point source:** spherical wavefronts; distant source: plane wavefronts.
- **Huygens' principle:** every point on a wavefront sends out secondary wavelets; their forward envelope is the new wavefront.
- **Speed in a denser medium (wave theory):** less than in a rarer medium.
- **On refraction:** frequency unchanged; wavelength and speed decrease in a denser medium.
- **Coherent sources:** sources with a constant phase difference.
- **Interference:** redistribution of light energy by superposition of two coherent waves.
- **Constructive interference:** path difference $n\lambda$.
- **Destructive interference:** path difference $(n+\frac12)\lambda$.
- **Fringe width:** distance between two consecutive bright or dark fringes.
- **Diffraction:** bending of light round the edges of an obstacle or aperture.
- **Single-slit minimum:** $a\sin\theta=n\lambda$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Phase difference and path difference
$\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta x$.
:::

:::formula Resultant intensity
$I=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\phi$; equal sources $I=4I_0\cos^2\dfrac\phi2$.
:::

:::formula YDSE fringe width
$\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$; angular width $\lambda/d$; in a medium $\beta/n$.
:::

:::formula YDSE fringe positions
Bright $y_n=\dfrac{n\lambda D}{d}$; dark $y_n=\left(n+\tfrac12\right)\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$.
:::

:::formula Width of the central maximum
Angular $\dfrac{2\lambda}{a}$; linear $\dfrac{2\lambda D}{a}$.
:::

:::formula Snell's law from wavefronts
$\dfrac{\sin i}{\sin r}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=n_{21}$.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Dv · Snell's law by Huygens (3 marks)
Diagram: AB, AC, wavelet radius $v_2\tau$, tangent CE, $i$, $r$ [1] → $BC=v_1\tau=AC\sin i$, $AE=v_2\tau=AC\sin r$ [1] → $\sin i/\sin r=v_1/v_2=n_{21}$ [1]
:::

:::skeleton Dv · Law of reflection by Huygens (3 marks)
Diagram with incident AB and reflected CE [1] → $BC=AE=v\tau$, common AC, right angles at B and E [1] → triangles congruent ⇒ $i=r$ [1]
:::

:::skeleton Cmp · Interference vs diffraction (2 marks)
Source of waves (two coherent sources vs one wavefront) [½] → fringe widths (equal vs central double) [½] → intensities (equal vs falling) [½] → dark fringes (perfect vs not) [½]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **YDSE centre:** always bright (zero path difference).
- **YDSE with white light:** central fringe white, others coloured.
- **YDSE in water:** fringe width decreases by factor n.
- **Red vs blue in YDSE:** red fringes are wider.
- **Slit separation doubled:** fringe width halves.
- **Two independent bulbs:** incoherent; no steady pattern.
- **Narrower single slit:** wider central maximum.
- **Central maximum vs other maxima:** twice as wide.
- **Phase difference π:** dark (destructive) for equal amplitudes.
- **Huygens' backward wavelets:** ignored (their effect cancels).

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Derivation not needed for β
For boards, YDSE fringe width is final expression only; spend the time on numericals.
:::

:::tip Angular vs linear width
Angular width is $\lambda/d$ (YDSE) or $2\lambda/a$ (central max); linear width multiplies by $D$.
:::

:::tip Units in YDSE
Convert mm to m and nm to m before using $\beta=\lambda D/d$.
:::

:::tip Polarisation is out
Polarisation (Malus's and Brewster's laws) is not in the 2026–27 syllabus.
:::
