## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Lorentz force:** $\vec F=q(\vec E+\vec v\times\vec B)$.
- **Magnetic force on a charge:** does no work; changes direction, not speed.
- **Tesla:** field that exerts 1 N on 1 C moving at 1 m s⁻¹ perpendicular to it.
- **Biot–Savart law:** field of a current element ∝ $I\,dl\sin\theta/r^2$.
- **Ampère's circuital law:** line integral of $\vec B$ round a closed loop is $\mu_0I_\text{enc}$.
- **Right-hand thumb rule:** thumb along current, curled fingers give the field direction.
- **Ampere:** current in two long parallel wires 1 m apart giving $2\times10^{-7}$ N m⁻¹ force.
- **Magnetic dipole moment of a loop:** $m=NIA$, along the normal by the right-hand rule.
- **Current sensitivity:** deflection per unit current, $NAB/k$.
- **Voltage sensitivity:** deflection per unit voltage, $NAB/kG$.
- **Radial field in a galvanometer:** keeps the coil's plane along B, so torque $=NIAB$.
- **Shunt:** small resistance in parallel with a galvanometer to make an ammeter.
- **Bohr magneton:** $eh/4\pi m_e=9.27\times10^{-24}$ A m².

## Key formulas

:::formula Magnetic force on a charge
$F=qvB\sin\theta$, $\vec F=q\vec v\times\vec B$.
:::

:::formula Radius and period in a magnetic field
$r=\dfrac{mv}{qB}=\dfrac{\sqrt{2mK}}{qB}$; $T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{qB}$ (independent of $v$).
:::

:::formula Force on a current-carrying wire
$\vec F=I\vec l\times\vec B$, $F=IlB\sin\theta$.
:::

:::formula Field at the centre of a circular coil
$B=\dfrac{\mu_0NI}{2R}$.
:::

:::formula Field on the axis of a circular loop
$B=\dfrac{\mu_0NIR^2}{2(R^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$.
:::

:::formula Field of a long straight wire
$B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$.
:::

:::formula Field inside a long solenoid
$B=\mu_0nI$, $n$ = turns per metre.
:::

:::formula Force per length between parallel wires
$\dfrac FL=\dfrac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$; like currents attract.
:::

:::formula Torque on a current loop
$\vec\tau=\vec m\times\vec B$, $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$; $m=NIA$.
:::

:::formula Shunt and series resistance
Ammeter $S=\dfrac{I_gG}{I-I_g}$ (parallel); voltmeter $R=\dfrac{V}{I_g}-G$ (series).
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Dv · Loop on the axis (3 marks)
Diagram, $r=\sqrt{R^2+x^2}$ [½] → $dB=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{I\,dl}{R^2+x^2}$ [½] → perpendicular parts cancel, axial parts $dB\sin\phi$ add [1] → $B=\dfrac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$ [1]
:::

:::skeleton Dv · Parallel wires and the ampere (3 marks)
$B_1=\mu_0I_1/2\pi d$ at wire 2 [½] → $F=I_2LB_1$ [½] → $F/L=\mu_0I_1I_2/2\pi d$, attraction for like currents (direction by rule) [1] → definition of ampere with $2\times10^{-7}$ N m⁻¹ [1]
:::

:::skeleton Dv · Galvanometer (3 marks)
Labelled diagram: coil, concave poles, soft-iron core, spring [1] → deflecting torque $NIAB$ (radial field) = restoring $k\phi$ [1] → $\phi=(NAB/k)I$; sensitivity $NAB/k$ [1]
:::

:::skeleton Dv · Electron's magnetic moment (2 marks)
$I=e/T=ev/2\pi r$, $A=\pi r^2$ [½] → $\mu=IA=evr/2$ [½] → $l=m_evr$ ⇒ $\mu=\dfrac{e}{2m_e}l$ [1]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Charge moving along B:** no magnetic force.
- **Work done by magnetic force:** zero.
- **Doubling speed in a uniform B:** radius doubles, period unchanged.
- **Path when v has a component along B:** helix.
- **Undeflected in crossed fields:** $v=E/B$.
- **Net force on a closed loop in a uniform field:** zero (torque may not be).
- **Field at the centre vs axis of a loop:** maximum at the centre.
- **Field outside a long solenoid:** nearly zero.
- **Doubling turns of a galvanometer:** current sensitivity doubles, voltage sensitivity unchanged.
- **Ideal ammeter / voltmeter resistance:** zero / infinite.
- **Equal and opposite antiparallel currents:** repel.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Electron direction
For negative charges the force $q\vec v\times\vec B$ reverses; apply the rule and flip it.
:::

:::tip Like currents attract
Parallel same-direction currents attract, unlike charges' behaviour; opposite currents repel.
:::

:::tip Angle in the torque formula
$\theta$ is between $\vec B$ and the normal to the loop, not the plane of the loop.
:::

:::tip Shunt placement
Shunt goes in parallel (small); voltmeter resistance goes in series (large).
:::

:::tip Solenoid is qualitative
For boards only $B=\mu_0nI$ and the field pattern are expected; no derivation via Ampère's loop.
:::
