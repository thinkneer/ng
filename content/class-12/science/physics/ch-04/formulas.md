# Ch 4 · Moving Charges and Magnetism — formulas

## Force on charges and wires

:::formula Lorentz force and circular motion
$\vec F=q(\vec E+\vec v\times\vec B)$;   $F_m=qvB\sin\theta$ (does no work)<br>
In $\vec B$: $r=\dfrac{mv}{qB}=\dfrac{\sqrt{2mK}}{qB}$;   $T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{qB}$ (independent of $v$)<br>
Velocity selector (crossed $E$, $B$): undeflected if $v=E/B$
:::

:::formula Force on a conductor
$\vec F=I\,\vec l\times\vec B$;   parallel wires $\dfrac FL=\dfrac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$ (like currents attract)
:::

## Fields of currents

:::formula Biot–Savart and Ampère
$d\vec B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{I\,d\vec l\times\hat r}{r^2}$;   $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I_\text{enc}$;   $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$ T m A⁻¹<br>
Loop centre: $B=\dfrac{\mu_0NI}{2R}$;   loop axis: $B=\dfrac{\mu_0NIR^2}{2(R^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$<br>
Long straight wire: $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$;   long solenoid (inside): $B=\mu_0nI$, ends $\tfrac12\mu_0nI$
:::

## Loop as a dipole and the galvanometer

:::formula Magnetic dipole and galvanometer
$\vec m=NI\vec A$;   $\vec\tau=\vec m\times\vec B$, $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$;   $U=-\vec m\cdot\vec B$<br>
Galvanometer (radial field): $NIAB=k\phi$;   current sensitivity $\dfrac{\phi}{I}=\dfrac{NAB}{k}$;   voltage sensitivity $\dfrac{\phi}{V}=\dfrac{NAB}{kG}$<br>
Ammeter: shunt $S=\dfrac{I_gG}{I-I_g}$ (parallel);   voltmeter: $R=\dfrac{V}{I_g}-G$ (series)
:::

- Revolving electron: $\mu_l=\dfrac{evr}{2}=\dfrac{e}{2m_e}l$; Bohr magneton $\mu_B=\dfrac{eh}{4\pi m_e}=9.27\times10^{-24}$ A m².
- Not in boards: cyclotron, toroid.
