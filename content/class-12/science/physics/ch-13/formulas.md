# Ch 13 · Nuclei — formulas

## Size, mass and binding energy

:::formula Nuclei
$A=Z+N$;   $R=R_0A^{1/3}$ ($R_0=1.2$ fm);   nuclear density ≈ $2.3\times10^{17}$ kg m⁻³ (same for all $A$)<br>
$E=mc^2$;   1 u $=1.6605\times10^{-27}$ kg $=931.5$ MeV<br>
Mass defect $\Delta M=[Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n]-M$;   $E_b=\Delta M\,c^2$;   $Q=(\Sigma m_\text{reactants}-\Sigma m_\text{products})c^2$
:::

- $E_b/A$ curve: ≈ 8 MeV for $30<A<170$; peak ≈ 8.75 MeV at $A=56$ (Fe). Low for light and heavy nuclei → fusion of light, fission of heavy nuclei releases energy.
- Nuclear force: strongest, short range (few fm), charge-independent, saturates; attractive beyond ~0.8 fm, repulsive below.

## Fission and fusion

- Fission: ${}^{235}_{92}\text{U}+{}^{1}_{0}\text{n}\to{}^{144}_{56}\text{Ba}+{}^{89}_{36}\text{Kr}+3\,{}^{1}_{0}\text{n}+{\approx}\,200$ MeV.
- Fusion (Sun): $4\,{}^{1}_{1}\text{H}+2e^-\to{}^{4}_{2}\text{He}+2\nu+6\gamma+26.7$ MeV.
- $Q>0$: exothermic. $Q$ = (total BE of products) − (total BE of reactants).
- Constants: $m_p=1.007276$ u, $m_n=1.008665$ u, $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$ mol⁻¹. Not in boards: radioactivity, decay law, isotopes/isobars as a topic.
