## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Nucleons:** protons and neutrons in a nucleus.
- **Atomic mass unit:** one-twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom, $1.66\times10^{-27}$ kg.
- **Nuclear radius:** $R=R_0A^{1/3}$, $R_0=1.2$ fm.
- **Nuclear density:** about $2.3\times10^{17}$ kg m⁻³, same for all nuclei.
- **Mass defect:** total mass of separate nucleons minus mass of the nucleus.
- **Binding energy:** energy needed to separate a nucleus into its nucleons, $\Delta Mc^2$.
- **BE per nucleon:** measure of stability; peak 8.75 MeV near A = 56.
- **Saturation of nuclear force:** each nucleon interacts only with its nearest neighbours.
- **Nuclear force:** strongest, short-range, charge-independent, repulsive below about 0.8 fm.
- **Fission:** splitting of a heavy nucleus into two medium nuclei with energy release.
- **Fusion:** joining of light nuclei into a heavier one with energy release.
- **Chain reaction:** neutrons from one fission cause further fissions.
- **Q-value:** energy released in a nuclear reaction, (mass lost) × $c^2$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Nuclear radius and density
$R=R_0A^{1/3}$; $\rho=\dfrac{3m}{4\pi R_0^3}$, independent of $A$.
:::

:::formula Mass defect
$\Delta M=[Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n]-M$.
:::

:::formula Binding energy
$E_b=\Delta M\,c^2$; 1 u ≡ 931.5 MeV.
:::

:::formula Q-value
$Q=(\Sigma m_\text{reactants}-\Sigma m_\text{products})c^2$; $Q>0$ exothermic.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- **Ratio of radii of nuclei with A = 27 and A = 64:** 3 : 4.
- **Neutron vs proton mass:** neutron slightly heavier.
- **Most stable nucleus region:** around A = 56 (iron).
- **Energy per fission of U-235:** about 200 MeV.
- **Energy from fusing four H into He:** about 26.7 MeV.
- **Why fusion needs ~10⁷ K:** nuclei need enough energy to overcome Coulomb repulsion.
- **Nuclear force between n–n, p–p, n–p:** about equal.
- **Range of nuclear force:** a few femtometres.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Nuclear mass vs atomic mass
If atomic masses are given, use the hydrogen atom mass in place of $m_p$ so the electrons cancel.
:::

:::tip Total vs per nucleon
Stability is judged by BE per nucleon, not total binding energy.
:::

:::tip Radioactivity is out
Radioactive decay law, half-life and α, β, γ decay are not in the 2026–27 syllabus.
:::

:::tip Unit conversion
1 MeV = $1.6\times10^{-13}$ J; 1 u = 931.5 MeV.
:::
