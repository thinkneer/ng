# Practical · Mitosis in onion root tip

The tip of a growing root is a **meristem**: its cells divide again and again, so a thin squash of it shows cells caught at every stage of mitosis. This is one of the commonest slide-preparation tasks in the practical exam. Mitosis itself is Class 11 theory; here you only need to prepare the slide, recognise the stages and explain each step.

## Aim and principle

**Aim.** To prepare a temporary mount of onion root tip to study mitosis.

**Principle.** The **root apical meristem** (about the first 1–2 mm behind the root cap) has actively dividing cells. Root tips are first **fixed** so the cells are killed with their chromosomes in place. Warm **dilute HCl** then **hydrolyses** (softens) the tissue and dissolves the middle lamella, so cells separate when squashed. A stain such as **aceto-carmine** colours the chromosomes deep red, so the stages can be seen. Onion (*Allium cepa*) has **2n = 16** large chromosomes, which makes it a good material.

**In one breath:** Root tips are meristems. Fix them, soften with warm HCl so cells come apart, stain chromosomes with aceto-carmine, squash and look for the stages; onion has 2n = 16.

## Materials

- Onion bulbs, a beaker or jar of water, blade or scissors, forceps, watch glass
- Fixative: **aceto-alcohol** (1 part glacial acetic acid : 3 parts ethanol)
- **1N HCl**; **2% aceto-carmine** stain; 45% acetic acid; a spirit lamp
- Slide, coverslip, needle, blotting paper, compound microscope

**In one breath:** Onion roots grown in water; aceto-alcohol to fix, 1N HCl to soften, aceto-carmine to stain; slide, coverslip, lamp and microscope.

## Procedure

```ng-steps
id: mitosis-procedure
title: Preparing the root-tip squash
step: **Grow roots**: keep an onion bulb on a jar of water, roots touching the water, for 3–6 days until roots are 2–3 cm long.
step: **Cut** 1–2 cm of the root tips in the morning, when cells divide actively.
step: **Fix** the tips in aceto-alcohol (1 : 3) for about 24 hours.
step: **Hydrolyse**: warm a tip in 1N HCl at about 60 °C for 5–10 minutes; this softens the tissue and separates cells.
step: **Stain**: put the tip in 2% aceto-carmine in a watch glass and warm gently (do not boil) for about 10 minutes.
step: **Mount**: cut the deeply stained tip (1–2 mm) on a slide, add a drop of 45% acetic acid and place a coverslip.
step: **Squash**: tap the coverslip gently with a needle, then press under blotting paper with the thumb, without sliding the coverslip.
step: **Observe** under low power, find the dividing zone, then use high power to identify the stages.
```

## Result and precautions

**Result.** The squash shows cells of the onion root tip in interphase and in the different stages of mitosis: prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.

**Precautions**
1. Cut root tips when cell division is active, and take only the **terminal 1–2 mm** for the squash.
2. Do not **boil** in HCl or stain; warm gently.
3. **Over-hydrolysis** makes the tissue mushy; under-hydrolysis stops cells separating.
4. Squash **gently and evenly**; never slide the coverslip.
5. Avoid **air bubbles**; remove extra stain with blotting paper.

**In one breath:** Result: all mitotic stages seen. Precautions: right time and only the tip, warm but never boil, correct hydrolysis, gentle even squash, no bubbles.
