# Ch 10 · Biotechnology and its Applications — key facts

:::formula Bt toxin pathway
Inactive protoxin → alkaline gut pH → active toxin → midgut pores → lysis → insect dies.
:::

:::formula RNAi pathway
Sense + anti-sense RNA → dsRNA → mRNA silenced → no protein → nematode dies.
:::

:::formula Insulin assembly
Chain A + chain B (made separately in *E. coli*) + disulphide bonds → human insulin (no C peptide to remove).
:::

## Transgenic animals: five uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Normal physiology and development | insulin-like growth factor |
| Study of disease | cancer, cystic fibrosis, rheumatoid arthritis, Alzheimer's |
| Biological products | α-1-antitrypsin (emphysema); Rosie's milk with human α-lactalbumin (2.4 g/L, 1997) |
| Vaccine safety | polio vaccine in mice |
| Chemical safety (toxicity) testing | sensitive transgenic animals |

## Facts to remember

- First gene therapy: 1990, 4-year-old girl, ADA deficiency, lymphocytes + retroviral vector, periodic infusions.
- Eli Lilly, 1983: recombinant human insulin.
- Over 95% of transgenic animals are mice.
- GEAC regulates GM research and release; Basmati patent 1997 (USA); 27 documented Basmati varieties in India.
