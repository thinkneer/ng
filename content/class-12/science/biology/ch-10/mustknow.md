## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Genetically modified organism:** organism whose genes have been altered by manipulation.
- **Golden rice:** GM rice rich in vitamin A.
- **Bt, full name:** *Bacillus thuringiensis*; makes insecticidal crystal proteins.
- **Insects killed by Bt toxins:** lepidopterans, coleopterans, dipterans.
- **Bt protoxin:** inactive form stored in crystals inside the bacterium.
- **Activation of Bt toxin:** alkaline pH of insect gut solubilises crystals.
- **Bt toxin action:** binds midgut epithelium, makes pores, cells swell and lyse.
- **cry genes:** genes coding Bt toxin proteins.
- **Cotton bollworm control:** cryIAc and cryIIAb.
- **Corn borer control:** cryIAb.
- **RNA interference (RNAi):** silencing of a specific mRNA by complementary dsRNA.
- **RNAi in nature:** cellular defence in all eukaryotes.
- **Nematode on tobacco roots:** *Meloidogyne incognita*.
- **RNAi vector for tobacco:** *Agrobacterium*.
- **dsRNA in transgenic tobacco:** formed from sense and anti-sense RNA of nematode genes.
- **Earlier insulin source:** pancreas of slaughtered cattle and pigs; caused allergy.
- **Insulin structure:** chains A and B linked by disulphide bridges.
- **Pro-insulin structure:** insulin precursor with an extra C peptide.
- **Recombinant insulin (1983):** Eli Lilly; A and B chains made separately in *E. coli*.
- **Gene therapy:** correcting a gene defect by inserting a normal gene.
- **First gene therapy (1990):** 4-year-old girl with ADA deficiency.
- **ADA enzyme:** adenosine deaminase; crucial for immune system function.
- **ADA gene therapy vector:** retrovirus carrying functional ADA cDNA into lymphocytes.
- **Permanent cure for ADA deficiency:** gene introduced at early embryonic stages.
- **Molecular probe:** labelled single-stranded DNA/RNA that hybridises to complementary DNA.
- **ELISA principle:** antigen–antibody interaction.
- **Transgenic animals:** animals carrying and expressing a foreign gene.
- **Commonest transgenic animal:** mouse, over 95%.
- **Use of α-1-antitrypsin:** human protein from transgenic animals; treats emphysema.
- **Rosie the cow:** first transgenic cow (1997); milk with human α-lactalbumin.
- **Vaccine tested in transgenic mice:** polio vaccine.
- **GEAC, full form and role:** Genetic Engineering Approval Committee; judges GM research and release safety.
- **Biopiracy, meaning:** using bio-resources without authorisation or compensation.
- **Basmati patent:** given to an American company in 1997.

## Key formulas

:::formula Bt toxin activation chain
Protoxin crystal → alkaline gut pH → active toxin → binds midgut cells → pores → swelling, lysis → death.
:::

:::formula RNAi chain
Sense RNA + anti-sense RNA → dsRNA → binds nematode mRNA → no translation → parasite dies.
:::

:::formula Rosie's milk
Human protein-enriched milk: 2.4 g per litre, with human α-lactalbumin.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Recombinant therapeutics approved worldwide: about 30; marketed in India: 12.
- Human insulin made in: *E. coli* plasmids.
- Other ADA treatments: bone marrow transplant, enzyme replacement therapy.
- PCR in diagnosis detects: HIV, cancer mutations.
- Disease models in transgenic animals: cancer, cystic fibrosis, rheumatoid arthritis, Alzheimer's.
- Growth factor studied in transgenics: insulin-like growth factor.
- Rice varieties in India: about 2,00,000; Basmati varieties documented: 27.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Bacterium is not killed
Bt toxin is an inactive protoxin inside the bacterium; only the alkaline insect gut activates it.
:::

:::tip Which cry gene
cryIAc and cryIIAb → cotton bollworm; cryIAb → corn borer.
:::

:::tip RNAi silences mRNA
RNAi blocks translation of the nematode's mRNA; it does not delete the nematode's DNA.
:::

:::tip C peptide
Recombinant insulin skips the C peptide: A and B chains are made separately and joined.
:::

:::tip Gene therapy cells
In the 1990 therapy, lymphocytes (not marrow stem cells) were engineered, so the cure was not permanent.
:::
