# Ch 11 · Organisms and Populations — relations and facts

## Population growth

:::formula Population change
$N_{t+1} = N_t + [(B + I) - (D + E)]$ — B natality, I immigration, D mortality, E emigration.
:::

:::formula Exponential growth
$\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN$ ⇒ $N_t = N_0\,e^{rt}$; $r = b - d$ (intrinsic rate of natural increase). J-shaped curve.
:::

:::formula Logistic growth
$\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\left(\dfrac{K - N}{K}\right)$; K = carrying capacity. S-shaped curve.
:::

:::formula Per capita rates
Birth rate = births ÷ N; death rate = deaths ÷ N (per unit time). Lotus: 8/20 = 0.4; fruit flies: 4/40 = 0.1.
:::

- r values: Norway rat 0.015 · flour beetle 0.12 · India (1981) 0.0205.

## Population interactions

| Interaction | A | B | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mutualism | + | + | lichen; mycorrhiza; fig–wasp; *Ophrys*–bee |
| Competition | − | − | flamingo and fish (zooplankton); *Balanus* vs *Chthamalus* |
| Predation | + | − | moth on prickly pear; *Pisaster* on invertebrates |
| Parasitism | + | − | liver fluke; *Cuscuta*; cuckoo (brood) |
| Commensalism | + | 0 | orchid on mango; egret and cattle; barnacle on whale |
| Amensalism | − | 0 | — |
