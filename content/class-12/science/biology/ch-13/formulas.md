# Ch 13 · Biodiversity and Conservation — relations and facts

:::formula Species–area relationship (Humboldt)
$\log S = \log C + Z \log A$, equivalently $S = CA^Z$. S = species richness, A = area, C = intercept, Z = slope.
:::

:::formula Z values
0.1–0.2 (most taxa and regions) · 0.6–1.2 (entire continents) · 1.15 (frugivorous birds and mammals, tropical forests).
:::

## Evil Quartet

| Cause | Example |
|---|---|
| Habitat loss and fragmentation | Amazon cleared for soya beans and beef cattle |
| Over-exploitation | Steller's sea cow, passenger pigeon |
| Alien species invasion | Nile perch (Lake Victoria cichlids); *Parthenium*, *Lantana*, water hyacinth; *Clarias gariepinus* |
| Co-extinction | host fish and its parasites; plant–pollinator pairs |
