# Ch 2 · Human Reproduction — key facts

## Gamete arithmetic

:::formula Sperms from spermatocytes
1 primary spermatocyte → 4 sperms; n primary spermatocytes → 4n sperms.
:::

:::formula Ova from oocytes
1 primary oocyte → 1 ovum + 2 or 3 polar bodies; n primary oocytes → n ova.
:::

:::formula Chromosome numbers
Spermatogonium, primary spermatocyte, oogonium, primary oocyte, zygote = 46 · secondary spermatocyte, spermatid, sperm, secondary oocyte, polar body, ovum = 23.
:::

## Menstrual cycle at a glance

- **Menstrual (days 1–5):** endometrium breaks down; progesterone has fallen.
- **Follicular (days 6–13):** follicle grows to Graafian; endometrium regenerates; FSH, LH rising; estrogen.
- **Ovulatory (about day 14):** ovulation; LH surge, FSH peak.
- **Luteal (days 15–28):** corpus luteum; endometrium maintained; progesterone.

## Hormones of reproduction

| Hormone | Source | Job |
|---|---|---|
| GnRH | hypothalamus | releases FSH and LH |
| FSH | anterior pituitary | Sertoli cells; follicle growth |
| LH | anterior pituitary | Leydig cells; LH surge → ovulation |
| Androgens | Leydig cells | spermatogenesis |
| Estrogen | growing follicles | endometrium regenerates |
| Progesterone | corpus luteum | maintains endometrium |
| hCG, hPL, estrogens, progestogens | placenta | maintain pregnancy |
| Relaxin | ovary (late pregnancy) | pregnancy-only |
| Oxytocin | maternal pituitary | uterine contractions at birth |

## Facts to remember

- Scrotum 2–2.5 °C cooler; ≈ 250 lobules; 1–3 tubules per lobule.
- 200–300 million sperms per ejaculate; ≥ 60% normal form, ≥ 40% vigorous motility.
- 60,000–80,000 primary follicles per ovary at puberty; no oogonia after birth.
- Morula = 8–16 blastomeres; blastocyst = trophoblast + inner cell mass.
- Milestones: heart 1 month · limbs 2 months · organ systems 12 weeks · movements 5th month · eyelashes 24 weeks · birth ≈ 9 months.
