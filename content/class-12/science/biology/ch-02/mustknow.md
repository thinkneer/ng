## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Role of scrotum:** keeps testes 2–2.5 °C below body temperature for spermatogenesis.
- **Testicular lobules:** about 250 per testis, each with 1–3 seminiferous tubules.
- **Sertoli cells:** nourish germ cells; acted on by FSH.
- **Leydig (interstitial) cells:** secrete androgens; acted on by LH.
- **Sperm duct path:** rete testis, vasa efferentia, epididymis, vas deferens, ejaculatory duct, urethra.
- **Seminal plasma:** rich in fructose, calcium and certain enzymes.
- **Bulbourethral glands:** secretion lubricates the penis.
- **Fimbriae of oviduct:** finger-like edges of infundibulum; collect the ovum after ovulation.
- **Oviduct parts (ovary → uterus):** infundibulum, ampulla, isthmus.
- **Uterine wall (out → in):** perimetrium, myometrium, endometrium.
- **Myometrium of uterus:** smooth muscle; strong contractions during delivery.
- **Endometrium of uterus:** glandular lining; cyclic changes in the menstrual cycle.
- **Birth canal:** cervical canal plus vagina.
- **Milk path:** alveoli → mammary tubules → mammary duct → mammary ampulla → lactiferous duct.
- **Spermiogenesis in testis:** spermatids transformed into spermatozoa.
- **Spermiation in testis:** release of sperms from the seminiferous tubules.
- **Acrosome of sperm:** cap on sperm head with enzymes for entering the ovum.
- **Sperm middle piece:** mitochondria that power tail movement.
- **Primary oocyte:** arrested in prophase I from foetal life.
- **Primary follicle:** primary oocyte with one layer of granulosa cells.
- **Tertiary follicle:** has a fluid-filled antrum; meiosis I completes here.
- **Zona pellucida:** membrane formed by the secondary oocyte in the Graafian follicle.
- **Ovulation in humans:** rupture of Graafian follicle releasing the secondary oocyte.
- **Menarche in girls:** first menstruation at puberty.
- **Menopause in women:** end of menstrual cycles, around 50 years.
- **LH surge:** mid-cycle peak of LH; causes ovulation.
- **Corpus luteum:** remains of Graafian follicle; secretes progesterone.
- **Site of fertilisation:** ampullary region of the fallopian tube.
- **Morula stage:** embryo with 8–16 blastomeres.
- **Blastocyst structure:** outer trophoblast plus inner cell mass.
- **Implantation of blastocyst:** blastocyst embedded in the endometrium.
- **Placenta structure:** chorionic villi interlocked with uterine tissue; foetus–mother exchange unit.
- **Pregnancy-only hormones:** hCG, hPL and relaxin.
- **Relaxin source:** ovary, late in pregnancy.
- **Foetal ejection reflex:** mild contractions that trigger oxytocin release at birth.
- **Colostrum (first milk):** rich in antibodies (IgA); protects the newborn.

## Key formulas

:::formula Products of one primary spermatocyte
1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 2 secondary spermatocytes (n) → 4 spermatids → 4 sperms.
:::

:::formula Products of one primary oocyte
1 primary oocyte (2n) → secondary oocyte (n) + 1st polar body → ovum (n) + 2nd polar body: 1 ovum only.
:::

:::formula Normal fertility numbers
200–300 million sperms per ejaculate; ≥ 60% normal shape and size; ≥ 40% vigorous motility.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Menstrual cycle (3 marks)
Menstrual: endometrium breaks down if ovum not fertilised [¾] → Follicular: follicle grows, endometrium regenerates, FSH/LH rise, estrogen [¾] → Ovulatory: LH surge, Graafian follicle ruptures, day 14 [¾] → Luteal: corpus luteum, progesterone maintains endometrium [¾]
:::

:::skeleton Oogenesis flowchart (3 marks)
Oogonia 2n (foetal ovary) [½] → primary oocyte 2n, arrested prophase I [½] → meiosis I in tertiary follicle, unequal [½] → secondary oocyte n + 1st polar body [½] → ovulation [½] → meiosis II after sperm entry → ovum + 2nd polar body [½]
:::

:::skeleton Fertilisation to implantation (3 marks)
Ampulla; zona pellucida blocks other sperms; acrosome enzymes; meiosis II completes [1] → cleavage → morula (8–16) → blastocyst: trophoblast + inner cell mass [1] → trophoblast attaches, uterine cells cover blastocyst = implantation [1]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Testis size: about 4–5 cm long, 2–3 cm wide.
- Ovary length: 2–4 cm.
- Oviduct length: 10–12 cm.
- Spermatogonium chromosome number: 46 (2n).
- Secondary spermatocyte chromosome number: 23 (n).
- Primary follicles left at puberty: 60,000–80,000 per ovary.
- Oogonia formed after birth: none.
- First polar body forms: in the tertiary follicle, before ovulation.
- Second polar body forms: at fertilisation, after sperm entry.
- Menstrual phase length: 3–5 days.
- Average cycle length: 28/29 days.
- Sex of the baby decided by: the father's sperm (X or Y).
- Hormone for spermatogenesis onset: rise in GnRH at puberty.
- Heart forms: after one month of pregnancy.
- Major organ systems formed: by end of 12 weeks (first trimester).
- First foetal movements: fifth month.
- Hormone injected to induce delivery: oxytocin.
- Hormones inducing parturition (NCERT): cortisol, estrogens and oxytocin.
- Ova released per month: one; fraternal twins need two.
- Hymen as indicator of virginity: not reliable.
- Germ layers from inner cell mass: ectoderm, endoderm, then mesoderm.
- Pregnancy test hormone: hCG.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Meiosis of the oocyte
Meiosis I ends before ovulation; meiosis II ends only after a sperm enters.
:::

:::tip Sertoli vs Leydig
Sertoli: inside tubule, nourish, FSH. Leydig: between tubules, androgens, LH.
:::

:::tip Site of fertilisation
Fertilisation is in the ampulla of the fallopian tube, not in the uterus.
:::

:::tip Corpus luteum hormone
Mainly progesterone, which maintains the endometrium; not mainly estrogen.
:::

:::tip Who becomes the embryo
The inner cell mass becomes the embryo; the trophoblast forms placental villi.
:::

:::tip Relaxin source
NCERT: relaxin is secreted by the ovary, not the placenta.
:::

:::tip Spermiogenesis vs spermiation
Spermiogenesis shapes the sperm; spermiation releases it from the tubule.
:::
