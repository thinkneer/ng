# Ch 3 · Reproductive Health — key facts

## Numbers to remember

:::formula Contraceptive time windows
Periodic abstinence days 10–17 · lactational amenorrhea ≤ 6 months · pill 21 on + 7 off · emergency ≤ 72 h.
:::

:::formula MTP time limits (NCERT)
≤ 12 weeks: one registered medical practitioner · 12–24 weeks: two registered medical practitioners.
:::

:::formula ART blastomere rule
ZIFT: ≤ 8 blastomeres → fallopian tube · IUT: > 8 blastomeres → uterus.
:::

## Contraceptives by group

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| Natural | periodic abstinence, withdrawal, lactational amenorrhea |
| Barrier | Nirodh (male condom), female condom, diaphragm, cervical cap, vault, spermicides |
| Non-medicated IUD | Lippes loop |
| Copper IUD | CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375 |
| Hormone IUD | Progestasert, LNG-20 |
| Pills | progestogen or progestogen–estrogen; Saheli (non-steroidal, weekly) |
| Injections, implants | progestogen ± estrogen |
| Surgical | vasectomy, tubectomy |

## ART at a glance

| Technique | Transferred → where |
|---|---|
| ZIFT | zygote / early embryo → fallopian tube |
| IUT | embryo > 8 cells → uterus |
| GIFT | donor ovum → fallopian tube |
| ICSI | sperm → injected into ovum |
| AI / IUI | semen → vagina / uterus |

## Facts to remember

- Family planning 1951 → RCH programmes; WHO: physical, emotional, behavioural, social well-being.
- Population: world 2 bn (1900) → 6 bn (2000) → 7.2 bn (2011); India 350 mn (1947) → 1.2 bn (2011).
- Marriage age 18 (female), 21 (male).
- MTP legal since 1971; 45–50 million MTPs/year worldwide.
- STIs: gonorrhoea, syphilis, genital herpes, chlamydiasis, genital warts, trichomoniasis, hepatitis-B, HIV; peak age 15–24.
