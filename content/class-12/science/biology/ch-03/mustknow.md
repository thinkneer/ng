## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Reproductive health (WHO):** total well-being in physical, emotional, behavioural and social aspects of reproduction.
- **Family planning in India:** started in 1951.
- **RCH programmes, full form:** Reproductive and Child Health Care programmes.
- **Amniocentesis test:** foetal sex test from chromosomal pattern in amniotic fluid.
- **Why amniocentesis is banned for sex determination:** to check female foeticide.
- **Ideal contraceptive:** user-friendly, available, effective, reversible, few side effects.
- **Periodic abstinence:** no coitus on days 10–17, the fertile period.
- **Coitus interruptus:** male withdraws before ejaculation.
- **Lactational amenorrhea:** no ovulation during intense breastfeeding; effective up to 6 months.
- **Condom (male brand):** Nirodh; also protects from STIs and AIDS.
- **Diaphragm, cervical cap, vault:** reusable rubber barriers covering the cervix.
- **Non-medicated IUD:** Lippes loop.
- **Copper-releasing IUDs:** CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375.
- **Hormone-releasing IUDs:** Progestasert, LNG-20.
- **Copper ions:** suppress sperm motility and fertilising capacity.
- **Hormone IUD extra action:** uterus unsuitable for implantation; cervix hostile to sperms.
- **Oral pill schedule:** 21 days from within first 5 days of cycle, then 7-day gap.
- **Pill action:** inhibits ovulation and implantation; alters cervical mucus.
- **Saheli oral pill:** non-steroidal once-a-week pill, CDRI Lucknow.
- **Emergency contraception:** within 72 hours of coitus.
- **Vasectomy procedure:** part of vas deferens removed or tied via scrotal incision.
- **Tubectomy procedure:** part of fallopian tube removed or tied via abdomen or vagina.
- **Surgical sterilisation:** terminal method; very poor reversibility.
- **Medical termination of pregnancy:** intentional termination of pregnancy before full term.
- **MTP legalised in India:** 1971.
- **MTP law (NCERT):** up to 12 weeks one RMP; 12–24 weeks two RMPs.
- **Safest period for MTP:** first trimester, up to 12 weeks.
- **STIs not completely curable:** hepatitis-B, genital herpes, HIV.
- **STI age group most affected:** 15–24 years.
- **Infertility in couples:** inability to conceive despite unprotected co-habitation.
- **ZIFT method:** zygote intra-fallopian transfer; embryo up to 8 blastomeres into the tube.
- **IUT method:** intra-uterine transfer; embryo of more than 8 blastomeres into uterus.
- **GIFT method:** gamete intra-fallopian transfer; donor ovum into the fallopian tube.
- **ICSI method:** intra-cytoplasmic sperm injection; sperm injected directly into the ovum.
- **IUI method:** intra-uterine insemination; semen introduced into the uterus.

## Key formulas

:::formula ZIFT or IUT by blastomere count
≤ 8 blastomeres → fallopian tube (ZIFT); > 8 blastomeres → uterus (IUT).
:::

:::formula Pill cycle
21 days on (start within days 1–5 of cycle) + 7 days off = one cycle, then repeat.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Population of India at independence: about 350 million.
- India crossed 1.2 billion: May 2011.
- Population growth rate 2011: below 2% (20 per 1000 per year).
- Legal marriageable age: 18 for females, 21 for males.
- Small-family slogan: Hum Do Hamare Do.
- MTPs worldwide: 45–50 million a year, about one-fifth of pregnancies.
- STI synonyms: venereal diseases (VD), reproductive tract infections (RTI).
- STI complications: PID, abortions, still births, ectopic pregnancy, infertility, cancer.
- Most widely accepted contraceptive in India: IUDs.
- Contraceptive side effects: nausea, abdominal pain, breakthrough bleeding, breast cancer.
- Natural methods: almost no side effects but high failure.
- Test-tube baby programme: IVF followed by embryo transfer.
- Best option for parenthood when ART fails or is costly: legal adoption.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Lippes loop
Lippes loop is non-medicated, not copper-releasing.
:::

:::tip Copper IUDs kill sperms
Write: copper ions suppress sperm motility and fertilising capacity; IUDs increase phagocytosis.
:::

:::tip ZIFT into the uterus
ZIFT goes into the fallopian tube; embryos above 8 blastomeres go into the uterus (IUT).
:::

:::tip GIFT transfers a zygote
GIFT transfers a gamete, a donor ovum; fertilisation happens in the body.
:::

:::tip Lactational amenorrhea for a year
It works only up to six months after delivery, with full breastfeeding.
:::

:::tip Emergency contraception window
Within 72 hours of coitus, not 7 days.
:::

:::tip AIDS is curable
Hepatitis-B, genital herpes and HIV are not completely curable.
:::
