# Ch 7 · Human Health and Disease — key facts

## Diseases at a glance

:::formula Malaria cycle in one line
Sporozoite (bite) → liver → RBC (haemozoin, fever every 3–4 days) → gametocyte → mosquito gut (fertilisation) → salivary gland.
:::

- **Typhoid:** *Salmonella typhi*; food, water (Widal test).
- **Pneumonia:** *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae*; droplets.
- **Common cold:** rhinoviruses; droplets, objects.
- **Malaria:** *Plasmodium* (*falciparum* = malignant); female *Anopheles*.
- **Amoebiasis:** *Entamoeba histolytica*; houseflies, food, water.
- **Ascariasis:** *Ascaris*; soil, water, vegetables.
- **Filariasis:** *Wuchereria bancrofti*, *W. malayi*; female mosquito.
- **Ringworm:** *Microsporum*, *Trichophyton*, *Epidermophyton*; towels, combs, soil.
- **Dengue, chikungunya:** viruses; *Aedes* mosquito.

## Immunity

| Type | Key facts |
|---|---|
| Innate | physical, physiological, cellular, cytokine (interferon) barriers |
| Acquired | memory; humoral (B cells, antibodies) and cell-mediated (T cells) |
| Active | own antibodies; vaccine or infection |
| Passive | ready-made antibodies; colostrum (IgA), antitoxin |

- Antibody = H₂L₂; types IgA, IgM, IgE, IgG. Allergy: IgE, mast cells, histamine, serotonin.
- Primary lymphoid organs: bone marrow, thymus. Secondary: spleen, lymph nodes, tonsils, Peyer's patches, appendix. MALT ≈ 50%.

## Drugs

| Drug | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Heroin (smack) | morphine from *Papaver somniferum* | depressant; opioid receptors |
| Marijuana, hashish, charas, ganja | *Cannabis sativa* | cardiovascular effects |
| Cocaine (coke, crack) | *Erythroxylum coca* | dopamine transport; CNS stimulant |
| Nicotine | tobacco | adrenaline, noradrenaline release |

## Facts to remember

- AIDS: HIV (retrovirus, RNA), first reported 1981; reverse transcriptase; macrophage = factory; helper T cells fall; ELISA.
- Cancer: contact inhibition lost; metastasis; carcinogens (X-rays, γ-rays, UV, tobacco smoke, oncogenic viruses); α-interferon in immunotherapy.
