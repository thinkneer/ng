# Ch 6 · Evolution — relations and facts

## Hardy–Weinberg

:::formula Hardy–Weinberg equation
$p + q = 1$ · $p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$ — $p^2$ = AA, $2pq$ = Aa, $q^2$ = aa.
:::

:::formula Allele frequency from homozygous recessives
$q = \sqrt{q^2}$, $p = 1 - q$. Example: aa = 1 in 400 → $q$ = 0.05, $p$ = 0.95, carriers $2pq$ ≈ 0.095.
:::

- Five factors that disturb equilibrium: gene flow, genetic drift, mutation, genetic recombination, natural selection.

## Homologous vs analogous

| Homologous (divergent) | Analogous (convergent) |
|---|---|
| forelimbs of whale, bat, cheetah, human | wings of butterfly and bird |
| vertebrate hearts, brains | eyes of octopus and mammal |
| thorn of *Bougainvillea*, tendril of *Cucurbita* | flippers of penguin and dolphin; potato and sweet potato |
