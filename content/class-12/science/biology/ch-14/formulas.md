# Practical · Pollen germination — relations and facts

## Pollen germination

:::formula Percentage germination
$\text{Germination (\%)}=\dfrac{\text{germinated}}{\text{total counted}}\times100$
:::

:::formula Germination medium
About 10% sucrose + a trace of boric acid in distilled water; observe after 15–30 minutes.
:::
