# Practical · Quadrat study — relations and facts

## Quadrat method

:::formula Population density
$\text{Density}=\dfrac{\text{total individuals}}{\text{quadrats studied}}$ (individuals per m²)
:::

:::formula Population frequency
$\text{Frequency (\%)}=\dfrac{\text{quadrats with species}}{\text{quadrats studied}}\times100$
:::
