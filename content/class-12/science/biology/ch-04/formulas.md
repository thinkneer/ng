# Ch 4 · Principles of Inheritance and Variation — ratios and facts

## Mendelian ratios

:::formula Monohybrid cross
F2 phenotype 3 : 1; genotype 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt. Test cross Tt × tt → 1 : 1.
:::

:::formula Dihybrid cross
F2 phenotype 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. Test cross AaBb × aabb → 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.
:::

:::formula Gametes from a genotype
Number of gamete types = 2ⁿ, where n = number of heterozygous gene pairs (AaBb → 4).
:::

:::formula Incomplete dominance
F2 = 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white (phenotype ratio = genotype ratio).
:::

:::formula X-linked recessive: carrier mother × normal father
Daughters: ½ normal, ½ carrier · sons: ½ normal, ½ affected.
:::

:::formula Autosomal recessive: carrier × carrier
¼ normal · ½ carrier · ¼ affected (sickle-cell, phenylketonuria, thalassaemia).
:::

## Facts to remember

- Linkage: white–yellow 1.3%; white–miniature 37.2%.
- Sickle-cell: GAG → GUG, Glu → Val, position 6 of β-globin.
- Thalassaemia: α (HBA1, HBA2, chromosome 16); β (HBB, chromosome 11).
- Colour blindness: 8% males, 0.4% females.
- Down's = trisomy 21 · Klinefelter's = 47, XXY · Turner's = 45, XO.
