## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Root tip for mitosis:** apical meristem with actively dividing cells.
- **Role of the fixative:** kills cells and preserves chromosomes as they are.
- **Fixative used:** aceto-alcohol, acetic acid : ethanol = 1 : 3.
- **Role of warm HCl:** softens tissue, dissolves middle lamella, separates cells.
- **HCl strength and temperature:** 1N HCl at about 60 °C.
- **Stain used:** 2% aceto-carmine (or aceto-orcein).
- **Role of aceto-carmine:** stains chromosomes deep red.
- **Mounting liquid:** 45% acetic acid.
- **Best stage to count chromosomes:** metaphase.
- **Onion chromosome number:** 2n = 16.
- **Commonest stage in a squash:** interphase.
- **Plant cell division end:** cell plate, not furrowing.

## Key formulas

:::formula Mitotic index
$\text{Mitotic index (\%)}=\dfrac{\text{dividing cells}}{\text{total cells counted}}\times100$

When to use: from the stage counts in your table. Unit: %. Common slip: counting interphase cells as dividing.
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Onion scientific name: *Allium cepa*.
- Chromosomes are best seen at: metaphase.
- Chromatids separate at: anaphase.
- Nuclear membrane re-forms at: telophase.
- Mitosis in plants is studied in: root and shoot tips.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Boiling the material
Boiling in HCl or stain spoils the chromosomes; warm gently.
:::

:::tip Sliding the coverslip
Sliding the coverslip while squashing breaks and smears the cells; press straight down.
:::
