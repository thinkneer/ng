## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Wind-pollinated flower features:** small, dull; light pollen in plenty; feathery stigma.
- **Insect-pollinated flower features:** large, bright, scented, with nectar.
- **Bird-pollinated flower features:** large, bright, much nectar.
- **T.S. testis key features:** seminiferous tubules; Sertoli cells; Leydig cells between tubules.
- **T.S. ovary key features:** follicles at different stages; Graafian follicle; corpus luteum.
- **Metaphase I identification:** bivalents at the equator.
- **Anaphase I identification:** homologous chromosomes move to opposite poles.
- **Blastula parts:** trophoblast, inner cell mass, blastocoel.
- **Controlled pollination steps:** emasculation, bagging, pollination, rebagging and tagging.
- **Ascaris disease and symptoms:** ascariasis; bleeding, pain, fever, anaemia, gut blockage.
- **Entamoeba disease and symptoms:** amoebiasis; cramps, mucus and blood in stools.
- **Ringworm fungi names:** *Microsporum*, *Trichophyton*, *Epidermophyton*.
- **Ringworm symptoms:** dry scaly skin lesions with intense itching.
- **Lichen association:** fungus with alga or cyanobacterium; mutualism.
- **Root nodule bacterium:** *Rhizobium* fixing nitrogen.
- **Cuscuta identification:** leafless, yellow, twining parasite with haustoria.

## Key formulas

:::formula Mendelian ratios for the seed spot
Monohybrid F2 = 3 : 1 · dihybrid F2 = 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 · test crosses 1 : 1 and 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.

When to use: comparing seed counts. Common slip: quoting the genotypic ratio 1 : 2 : 1 for phenotypes.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Any spot (2–3 points)
Identification (name) [1] → two or three visible features (½ each) → one function or disease where asked [½]
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- *Ascaris* male tail: curved; female tail: straight.
- *Cuscuta* lacks: chlorophyll and leaves.
- Lichens are indicators of: low air pollution (absent where SO₂ is high).
- Example of a wind-pollinated plant: maize.
- Example of a bird-pollinated plant: *Bombax* (silk cotton).

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Name without points
A correct name alone earns only part of the spot's marks; always add two visible features.
:::

:::tip Meiosis I vs II
In anaphase I whole homologous chromosomes separate; in anaphase II sister chromatids separate.
:::
