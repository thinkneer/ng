# Ch 9 · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes — relations and facts

:::formula PCR doubling
Copies after n cycles = 2ⁿ × starting copies; about 30 cycles ≈ 10⁹ (a billion) copies.
:::

:::formula Electrophoresis
DNA (–) moves to anode (+); distance travelled ∝ 1/size (smaller goes farther).
:::

:::formula EcoRI site
5′-G↓AATTC-3′ / 3′-CTTAA↑G-5′ → sticky ends AATT.
:::

## Steps of recombinant DNA technology

1. Isolate DNA (lysozyme / cellulase / chitinase; RNase; protease; chilled ethanol).
2. Cut with restriction enzyme (check by gel electrophoresis); cut vector with the same enzyme.
3. Amplify gene by PCR (denature → anneal → extend; Taq polymerase).
4. Ligate insert into vector (DNA ligase).
5. Transfer into competent host; select transformants (antibiotic, blue–white).
6. Express and scale up (bioreactor, continuous culture) → downstream processing.
