# Practical · Project, record and exam day — must know

## Most-repeated board questions

Stars are estimates from practical-exam viva patterns, not exact counts: ★★★ most years · ★★ often · ★ sometimes.

1. ★★★ What was the aim of your project, and what did you find? [viva]
2. ★★★ Which factor did you change, and what did you keep constant? [viva]
3. ★★ What are the sources of error in your project? [viva]
4. ★★ How is your project linked to the syllabus? [viva]
5. ★ What would you change if you repeated it? [viva]

## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Practical marks split:** experiments 5 + 4, slide 5, spotting 7, record + viva 4, project 5.
- **Practical exam window:** 1 January to 13 February 2027.
- **Investigatory project aim:** one clear question answered by your own observations.
- **Fair test rule:** change one factor; measure one result; keep the rest constant.
- **Repeat readings reason:** averages reduce chance errors.
- **Project file order:** title, certificate, index, aim, theory, method, observations, result, bibliography.
- **Experiment write-up order:** aim, principle, materials, procedure, diagram, table, result, precautions.
- **Record diagrams drawn in:** pencil, on the plain page, labelled.
- **Spotting answer pattern:** name first, then two or three visible features.
- **First step on exam day:** start the experiment step that needs waiting time.

## Key formulas

:::formula Percentage change
$\text{Change (\%)}=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{old}}{\text{old}}\times100$

When to use: comparing a treatment with the control in a project. Unit: %. Common slip: dividing by the new value.
:::

:::formula Average of repeats
$\bar{x}=\dfrac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3}$

When to use: three repeat readings of one treatment. Common slip: averaging readings from different treatments.
:::

## Answer skeletons

:::skeleton Two-minute project explanation (viva)
Aim in one sentence → why it matters (syllabus link) → what you changed, measured, kept constant → main result with a number → one source of error and one improvement
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- The factor you change in an experiment: the independent variable.
- The result you measure: the dependent variable.
- The set-up without the treatment: the control.
- Major experiment carries: 5 marks; minor experiment: 4 marks.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Unsigned record
An unsigned or incomplete practical record can cost the record marks; get each experiment signed as you go.
:::

:::tip Copied project
A project copied from the internet or a senior shows in the viva; do your own small study instead of a big borrowed one.
:::
