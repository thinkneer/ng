# Ch 12 · Ecosystem — relations and facts

:::formula Net primary productivity
NPP = GPP − R; units g m⁻² yr⁻¹ or kcal m⁻² yr⁻¹.
:::

:::formula 10 per cent law (Lindeman)
Energy passed to next trophic level ≈ 10%: E(n+1) = 0.1 × E(n). After k transfers: E = E₀ × (0.1)ᵏ.
:::

:::formula Sunlight to plants
Incident radiation → PAR (< 50%) → captured by plants (2–10% of PAR).
:::

- Global NPP ≈ 170 billion tons (dry weight) per year; oceans ≈ 55 billion tons.

## Decomposition

| Step | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Fragmentation | detritivores break detritus | on detritus |
| Leaching | soluble nutrients sink as unavailable salts | soil |
| Catabolism | enzymes of bacteria, fungi degrade | on detritus |
| Humification | humus forms (slow to decompose) | soil |
| Mineralisation | inorganic nutrients released | soil |

- Faster: nitrogen-rich, sugar-rich detritus; warm, moist. Slower: lignin, chitin; cold; anaerobic.

## Pyramids

| Pyramid | Usually | Inverted when |
|---|---|---|
| Numbers | upright | tree with many insects |
| Biomass | upright | sea (phytoplankton < fish) |

- Limitations: same species at two levels ignored; simple chains only, no food web; saprophytes left out.
