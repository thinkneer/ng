# Ch 8 · Application of Integrals — formulas and key facts

## Setting up the area

:::formula Area as an integral
Vertical strips: $A=\displaystyle\int_a^by\,dx$ (between $x=a$, $x=b$ and the $x$-axis)<br>
Horizontal strips: $A=\displaystyle\int_c^dx\,dy$ (between $y=c$, $y=d$ and the $y$-axis)<br>
Below the axis: take $\left|\displaystyle\int\right|$ for that piece; split at every crossing or corner
:::

- Circle and ellipse centred at $O$: $4\times$ first-quadrant area. $y^2=4ax$: $2\times$ upper half.
- Line with a curve: find the intersection, split where the boundary changes, add.
- Triangle by integration: areas under the two upper sides minus the area under the lower side.

## Integrals used

:::formula Standard integrals for this chapter
$\displaystyle\int\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\,dx=\dfrac x2\sqrt{a^2-x^2}+\dfrac{a^2}{2}\sin^{-1}\dfrac xa+C$<br>
$\displaystyle\int\sqrt x\,dx=\dfrac23x^{3/2}+C$
:::

## Curves to sketch

| Equation | Shape |
|---|---|
| $x^2+y^2=a^2$ | circle, centre $O$, radius $a$ |
| $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ | ellipse through $(\pm a,0)$, $(0,\pm b)$ |
| $y^2=4ax$ | parabola opening right, focus $(a,0)$, latus rectum $x=a$ |
| $x^2=4ay$ | parabola opening up |
| $y=\lvert x-a\rvert$ | V with corner at $(a,0)$ |
| $y=mx+c$ | line; mark both intercepts |

## Standard areas (to check an answer)

| Region | Area (sq. units) |
|---|---|
| Circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$ | $\pi a^2$ |
| Ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ | $\pi ab$ |
| $y^2=4ax$ and its latus rectum $x=a$ | $\dfrac83a^2$ |
| $y^2=4ax$ and the line $x=h$ | $\dfrac83\sqrt a\,h^{3/2}$ |
| $x^2=4ay$ and the line $y=a$ | $\dfrac83a^2$ |
| Circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$, smaller part cut by $x=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt2}$ | $\dfrac{a^2}{2}\left(\dfrac\pi2-1\right)$ |
| $y=\sin x$, $0$ to $\pi$ | $2$ |
| $y=\sin x$ or $\cos x$, $0$ to $2\pi$ | $4$ |
