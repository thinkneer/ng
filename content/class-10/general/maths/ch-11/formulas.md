# Ch 11 · Areas Related to Circles — formulas and key facts

Circle of radius $r$; sector angle $\theta$ in degrees.

:::formula Sector and arc
Area of sector $=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times\pi r^2$<br>
Length of arc $=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times2\pi r$<br>
Perimeter of sector $=$ arc $+\,2r$<br>
Major sector $=\pi r^2-$ minor sector
:::

:::formula Segment
Minor segment $=$ sector $-$ triangle $OAB$<br>
Major segment $=\pi r^2-$ minor segment
:::

| Angle at centre | Area of triangle OAB |
|---|---|
| 90° | $\dfrac12r^2$ |
| 60° | $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$ |
| 120° | $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$ |

- Minute hand: 6° per minute. Hour hand: 30° per hour.
- Fractions: 90° = ¼, 60° = ⅙, 120° = ⅓, 45° = ⅛, 30° = 1/12 of the circle.
