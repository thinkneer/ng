## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Sector of a circle:** region bounded by two radii and an arc.
- **Segment of a circle:** region bounded by a chord and an arc.
- **Minor sector:** the smaller sector, with angle $\theta$.
- **Major sector:** the rest of the circle, with angle $360^\circ-\theta$.
- **Quadrant of a circle:** a sector of angle $90^\circ$, one-fourth of the circle.
- **Minute hand speed:** $6^\circ$ per minute.
- **Hour hand speed:** $30^\circ$ per hour, that is $0.5^\circ$ per minute.
- **Major segment area:** area of circle minus minor segment.
- **Major sector area:** area of circle minus minor sector.
- **Triangle for 90° at the centre:** area $\dfrac12r^2$.
- **Triangle for 60° at the centre:** equilateral, area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$.
- **Triangle for 120° at the centre:** area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Area of a sector
$\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times\pi r^2$
:::

:::formula Length of an arc
$\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times2\pi r$
:::

:::formula Area of a minor segment
sector − triangle $=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2-\text{area of }\triangle OAB$
:::

:::formula Perimeter of a sector
$\dfrac{\theta}{360}\times2\pi r+2r$
:::

:::formula Sector area from arc length
$\dfrac12\times\text{arc length}\times r$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Sector of radius 6 cm and angle 60°: area $6\pi$ cm².
- Arc of radius 21 cm and angle 120°: length 44 cm.
- Area of a quadrant of a circle of radius 7 cm: 38.5 cm².
- Angle swept by the minute hand in 20 minutes: $120^\circ$.
- Perimeter of a quadrant of radius 7 cm: 25 cm.
- Angle of each sector when a circle is cut into 8 equal parts: $45^\circ$.
- Area of a semicircle of radius $r$: $\dfrac12\pi r^2$.
- Perimeter of a semicircle of radius $r$: $\pi r+2r$.
- If the arc length equals the radius, sector area is: $\dfrac12r^2$.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sector for segment
"Segment" needs the triangle subtracted from the sector.
:::

:::tip Perimeter without radii
Perimeter of a sector = arc + two radii.
:::

:::tip Diameter used as radius
Halve the diameter before you substitute.
:::

:::tip Wrong value of π
Use the value given in the question; state it if you choose $\dfrac{22}{7}$.
:::

:::tip Units of area and length
Area in cm² or m²; arc length and perimeter in cm or m.
:::
