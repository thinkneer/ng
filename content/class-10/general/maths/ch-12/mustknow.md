## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Slant height of a cone:** $l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$.
- **Curved surface of a cylinder:** $2\pi rh$.
- **Curved surface of a cone:** $\pi rl$.
- **Curved surface of a hemisphere:** $2\pi r^2$.
- **Total surface of a solid hemisphere:** $3\pi r^2$.
- **Surface area of a sphere:** $4\pi r^2$.
- **Surface area of a combination:** only the visible surfaces are added.
- **Volume of a combination:** the volumes of the parts are added.
- **Solid with a cavity:** subtract the volume of the cavity.
- **Height of a hemisphere:** equal to its radius.
- **Cone and cylinder, same base and height:** cone is one-third of the cylinder.
- **One litre in cubic centimetres:** 1000 cm³.
- **One cubic metre in litres:** 1000 litres.
- **Frustum of a cone:** not in boards.

:::formula Volume of a sphere
$\dfrac43\pi r^3$
:::

:::formula Volume of a hemisphere
$\dfrac23\pi r^3$
:::

:::formula Surface of a cone on a hemisphere
$\pi rl+2\pi r^2$
:::

:::formula Surface of a capsule
$2\pi rh+4\pi r^2$
:::

:::formula Surface of a cube with a hemisphere on it
$6a^2+\pi r^2$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Two cubes of edge 3 cm joined end to end: surface area 90 cm².
- Slant height of a cone with $r=5$, $h=12$: 13 cm.
- Cylinder, hemisphere, cone of same radius, height $=r$: volumes $3:2:1$.
- Largest sphere carved from a cube of edge $a$: radius $\dfrac a2$.
- Largest cone carved from a cylinder: same base and same height.
- Two solid hemispheres of radius $r$ joined at their bases: surface $4\pi r^2$.
- Radius doubled, height unchanged: volume of a cylinder becomes four times.
- A sphere and the cylinder that just encloses it: volumes $2:3$.
- Number of faces lost when two cubes are joined: two (one from each).

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Adding total surface areas
Joined faces are not on the surface: do not add TSA + TSA.
:::

:::tip Total height used as cone height
Subtract the hemisphere's radius from the total height first.
:::

:::tip Diameter for radius
Halve the diameter before substituting.
:::

:::tip Height for slant height
$\pi rl$ needs the slant height $l$, not $h$.
:::

:::tip Wrong units
Area in square units, volume in cubic units; convert to litres only at the end.
:::
