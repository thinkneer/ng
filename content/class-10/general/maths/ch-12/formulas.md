# Ch 12 · Surface Areas and Volumes — formulas and key facts

$r$ radius · $h$ height · $l$ slant height · $a$ edge

| Solid | Curved surface | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Cylinder | $2\pi rh$ | $\pi r^2h$ |
| Cone | $\pi rl$ | $\dfrac13\pi r^2h$ |
| Sphere | $4\pi r^2$ | $\dfrac43\pi r^3$ |
| Hemisphere | $2\pi r^2$ | $\dfrac23\pi r^3$ |
| Cuboid | $2h(l+b)$ | $lbh$ |
| Cube | $4a^2$ | $a^3$ |

:::formula Total surface areas
Cylinder $2\pi r(r+h)$ · Cone $\pi r(l+r)$ · Solid hemisphere $3\pi r^2$<br>
Cuboid $2(lb+bh+hl)$ · Cube $6a^2$<br>
Cone: $l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$
:::

:::formula Combinations
Cone on hemisphere: $\pi rl+2\pi r^2$<br>
Capsule: $2\pi rh+4\pi r^2$<br>
Tent (canvas): $\pi rl+2\pi rh$<br>
Cube with hemisphere on top or scooped out: $6a^2+\pi r^2$<br>
Volume: add the parts; subtract a cavity
:::

- 1 litre = 1000 cm³; 1 m³ = 1000 litres; 1 m² = 10 000 cm².
