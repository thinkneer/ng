# Ch 7 · Coordinate Geometry — formulas and key facts

:::formula Distance
$PQ=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$<br>
From the origin: $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$
:::

:::formula Section formula (internal, AP : PB = m : n)
$x=\dfrac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n}$<br>
$y=\dfrac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}$
:::

:::formula Midpoint
$\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2},\ \dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$
:::

## Tests

| To show | Check |
|---|---|
| Collinear points | two distances add up to the third |
| Isosceles triangle | two sides equal |
| Right triangle | longest side² = sum of the other two squares |
| Parallelogram | opposite sides equal (or diagonals have the same midpoint) |
| Rectangle | opposite sides equal and diagonals equal |
| Rhombus | four sides equal |
| Square | four sides equal and diagonals equal |

- Unknown ratio: take $k:1$. Point on the x-axis: $(x,0)$; on the y-axis: $(0,y)$.
- Trisection points divide the segment in $1:2$ and $2:1$.
