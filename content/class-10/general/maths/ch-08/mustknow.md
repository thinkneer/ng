## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Sine of angle θ:** opposite side ÷ hypotenuse.
- **Cosine of angle θ:** adjacent side ÷ hypotenuse.
- **Tangent of angle θ:** opposite side ÷ adjacent side.
- **Reciprocal ratios:** cosec = 1/sin, sec = 1/cos, cot = 1/tan.
- **Hypotenuse of a right triangle:** the side opposite the right angle; the longest side.
- **Opposite and adjacent sides:** depend on which acute angle you choose.
- **Why ratios depend only on the angle:** right triangles with the same angle are similar.
- **Largest value of sin θ or cos θ:** 1, so $\sin\theta=\dfrac43$ is impossible.
- **Trigonometric identity:** an equation true for every value of the angle.
- **Meaning of $\sin^2A$:** $(\sin A)^2$, not $\sin(A^2)$.
- **Not in boards 2026–27:** ratios of complementary angles, such as $\sin(90^\circ-A)=\cos A$.

## Key formulas

:::formula Tangent in terms of sine and cosine
$\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$,  $\cot\theta=\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$
:::

:::formula First identity (sine and cosine)
$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$
:::

:::formula Second identity (secant and tangent)
$1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$
:::

:::formula Third identity (cosecant and cotangent)
$1+\cot^2\theta=\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta$
:::

:::formula Sine of 30°, 45°, 60°
$\dfrac12,\ \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
:::

:::formula Cosine of 30°, 45°, 60°
$\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\ \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},\ \dfrac12$
:::

:::formula Tangent of 30°, 45°, 60°
$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3},\ 1,\ \sqrt3$
:::

:::formula Values at 0° and 90°
$\sin0^\circ=0,\ \cos0^\circ=1,\ \tan0^\circ=0$;  $\sin90^\circ=1,\ \cos90^\circ=0,\ \tan90^\circ$ not defined
:::

:::formula Sides of the 30°–60°–90° triangle
$1:\sqrt3:2$ (opposite 30°, opposite 60°, hypotenuse)
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Value of $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta$ for any θ: always 1.
- Value of $\sec^2A-\tan^2A$: always 1, so $9\sec^2A-9\tan^2A=9$.
- Value of $\operatorname{cosec}^2A-\cot^2A$: always 1.
- Angle at which $\sin\theta=\cos\theta$: 45°.
- Value of $\dfrac{1-\tan^245^\circ}{1+\tan^245^\circ}$: equals 0.
- Value of $\dfrac{2\tan30^\circ}{1+\tan^230^\circ}$: equals $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, which is $\sin60^\circ$.
- As θ goes from 0° to 90°, sin θ: increases from 0 to 1.
- As θ goes from 0° to 90°, cos θ: decreases from 1 to 0.
- The statement $\sin(A+B)=\sin A+\sin B$: false in general.
- Simplified form of $(1+\tan^2A)\cos^2A$: equals 1.
- Product $\tan A\times\cot A$: always 1.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Opposite and adjacent swapped
Mark the angle first; the opposite side faces it.
:::

:::tip Cancelling the angle
$\dfrac{\sin A}{\cos A}$ is $\tan A$, not $\dfrac{\sin}{\cos}$ with $A$ cancelled.
:::

:::tip tan 90° written as 0 or ∞
Write "not defined".
:::

:::tip Cross-multiplying a proof
Work on one side only; cross-multiplying assumes what you must prove.
:::

:::tip Surd in the denominator
Rationalise the final answer: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$ when the question asks for it.
:::

:::tip Missing last line
End every proof with "= RHS. Hence proved."
:::
