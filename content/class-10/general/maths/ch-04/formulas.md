# Ch 4 · Quadratic Equations — formulas and key facts

Standard form: $ax^2+bx+c=0$, $a\ne0$.

## Solving

:::formula Methods
Factorisation: split the middle term (two numbers with product $ac$ and sum $b$); each factor $=0$.<br>
Quadratic formula: $x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$
:::

## Discriminant $D=b^2-4ac$

| $D$ | Roots | Graph |
|---|---|---|
| $D>0$ | two distinct real | cuts the $x$-axis twice |
| $D=0$ | two equal, $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$ | touches the $x$-axis |
| $D<0$ | no real roots | does not meet it |

Sum of roots $=-\dfrac ba$; product of roots $=\dfrac ca$.

## Word-problem set-ups

| Situation | Equation |
|---|---|
| Consecutive integers | $x(x+1)=$ product |
| Rectangle | length × breadth = area |
| Speed changes by $k$ | $\dfrac{d}{x}-\dfrac{d}{x+k}=$ time saved |
| Boat, stream speed $x$ | $\dfrac{d}{u-x}-\dfrac{d}{u+x}=$ time difference |
| Two taps | $\dfrac1x+\dfrac{1}{x-k}=\dfrac1T$ |
| Right triangle | (side)² + (side)² = (hypotenuse)² |

Reject roots that are negative or otherwise impossible, and say why.

Not in boards 2026–27: solving a quadratic by completing the square (as a separate method).
