## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Quadratic equation:** $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with $a\ne0$.
- **Root of an equation:** a value of $x$ that satisfies it.
- **Roots and zeros:** roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ are the zeros of $ax^2+bx+c$.
- **Number of roots:** a quadratic equation has at most two.
- **Discriminant of a quadratic:** $D=b^2-4ac$.
- **Equal roots condition:** $D=0$; each root is $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$.
- **Real roots condition:** $D\ge0$.
- **Zero-product rule:** if $pq=0$ then $p=0$ or $q=0$.
- **Sum and product of roots:** $-\dfrac ba$ and $\dfrac ca$, as for zeros.
- **Not in boards 2026–27:** solving by completing the square as a separate method.

## Key formulas

:::formula Quadratic formula
$x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$
:::

:::formula Discriminant
$D=b^2-4ac$
:::

:::formula Nature of roots when D > 0
two distinct real roots
:::

:::formula Nature of roots when D = 0
two equal real roots, each $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$
:::

:::formula Nature of roots when D < 0
no real roots
:::

:::formula Time from distance and speed
$\text{time}=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$
:::

:::formula Two taps filling together
$\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y=\dfrac1T$ ($x$, $y$ hours alone; $T$ hours together)
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Roots of $x^2-3x-10=0$: $5$ and $-2$.
- Discriminant of $2x^2-4x+3=0$: $-8$, so no real roots.
- $2x^2-kx+k=0$ has equal roots for: $k=0$ or $k=8$.
- If 3 is a root of $x^2-2kx-6=0$: $k=\dfrac12$.
- Roots of $4x^2-12x+9=0$: equal, each $\dfrac32$.
- Roots of $x^2+x+1=0$: not real, because $D=-3$.
- An equation whose $x^2$ terms cancel: is not quadratic.
- Roots of $x^2=5x$: $0$ and $5$.
- If $a$ and $c$ have opposite signs: the roots are always real.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sign of −b
With $b=-5$, $-b=+5$. Use brackets when substituting.
:::

:::tip Dividing by x
Cancelling $x$ loses the root $x=0$; factorise instead.
:::

:::tip Root not rejected
State why the negative or fractional root is not possible.
:::

:::tip Standard form skipped
Read $a$, $b$, $c$ only after writing $ax^2+bx+c=0$.
:::

:::tip k that kills the quadratic
For "find $k$", reject any value that makes the coefficient of $x^2$ zero.
:::

:::tip Half the formula
The whole of $-b\pm\sqrt D$ is divided by $2a$, not just the root.
:::
