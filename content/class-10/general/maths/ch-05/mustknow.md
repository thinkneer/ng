## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Arithmetic progression:** a list in which each term is the previous term plus a fixed number.
- **Common difference $d$:** any term minus the term before it.
- **General form of an AP:** $a,\ a+d,\ a+2d,\ \dots$
- **Three numbers in AP:** take them as $a-d,\ a,\ a+d$.
- **Condition for $a,b,c$ in AP:** $2b=a+c$.
- **A number is a term of the AP:** only if $n$ comes out a positive whole number.
- **Term or sum in a word problem:** one stage needs $a_n$; "in all" needs $S_n$.
- **Graph of the terms of an AP:** points on a straight line of slope $d$.
- **Sum of first $n$ odd numbers:** $n^2$.

## Key formulas

:::formula nth term of an AP
$a_n=a+(n-1)d$
:::

:::formula Sum of n terms (first term and common difference)
$S_n=\dfrac n2\left[2a+(n-1)d\right]$
:::

:::formula Sum of n terms (first and last term)
$S_n=\dfrac n2\,(a+l)$
:::

:::formula A term from two sums
$a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$
:::

:::formula Sum of the first n positive integers
$\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$
:::

:::formula Number of terms from first and last term
$n=\dfrac{l-a}{d}+1$
:::

:::formula nth term from the end
$l-(n-1)d$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Common difference of $3,\ 1,\ -1,\ -3$: $-2$.
- 30th term of $10,\ 7,\ 4,\ \dots$: $-77$.
- Number of terms in $7,\ 13,\ 19,\ \dots,\ 205$: 34.
- Sum of the first 100 natural numbers: 5050.
- $2k,\ k+10,\ 3k+2$ are in AP when: $k=6$.
- Number of two-digit numbers divisible by 3: 30.
- Sum of first $n$ odd natural numbers: $n^2$.
- The list $0.2,\ 0.22,\ 0.222$: is not an AP.
- If $d=0$, every term of the AP: equals the first term.
- Sum of first 15 multiples of 8: 960.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip n − 1, not n
The $n$th term has $(n-1)$ steps of $d$.
:::

:::tip Sign of d
$d$ = next term − previous term; a falling AP has negative $d$.
:::

:::tip Term or sum
"In the 10th week" is a term; "in 10 weeks" is a sum.
:::

:::tip Two values of n
Reject negative or fractional $n$; keep both if both are positive whole numbers, with a reason.
:::

:::tip Bracket in the sum formula
It is $\dfrac n2[2a+(n-1)d]$: the $\dfrac n2$ multiplies the whole bracket.
:::
