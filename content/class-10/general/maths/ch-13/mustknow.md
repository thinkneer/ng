## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Class mark:** half the sum of the lower and upper class limits.
- **Class size:** upper limit minus lower limit.
- **Cumulative frequency:** running total of the frequencies.
- **Modal class:** the class with the highest frequency.
- **Median class:** the first class whose cumulative frequency reaches $\dfrac n2$.
- **cf in the median formula:** cumulative frequency of the class before the median class.
- **Symbols f0 and f2:** frequencies of the classes before and after the modal class.
- **Assumed mean a:** a convenient class mark, usually the middle one.
- **Deviation $d_i$:** $x_i-a$.
- **Step deviation $u_i$:** $\dfrac{x_i-a}{h}$.
- **Measure affected most by extreme values:** the mean.
- **Three methods of mean:** all give the same answer.
- **Ogive questions:** not in boards.

## Key formulas

:::formula Mean: direct method
$\bar x=\dfrac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i}$
:::

:::formula Mean: assumed mean method
$\bar x=a+\dfrac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i}$
:::

:::formula Mean: step-deviation method
$\bar x=a+\dfrac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}\times h$
:::

:::formula Mode of grouped data
$l+\dfrac{f_1-f_0}{2f_1-f_0-f_2}\times h$
:::

:::formula Median of grouped data
$l+\dfrac{\frac n2-cf}{f}\times h$
:::

:::formula Empirical relationship
$3\ \text{Median}=\text{Mode}+2\ \text{Mean}$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Class mark of the class 10–25: 17.5.
- If $\sum f_i=40$ and $\sum f_ix_i=1120$, the mean is: 28.
- If median = 26 and mode = 29, the mean is about: 24.5.
- If mean = 27 and median = 28, the mode is about: 30.
- The mode of grouped data lies: inside the modal class.
- $\sum f_i(x_i-\bar x)$ always equals: 0.
- The median divides the data into: two equal halves.
- In the mode formula, $h$ is: the size of the modal class.
- Median uses a column of: cumulative frequencies.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Wrong cf
Use the cf of the class before the median class, not of the median class.
:::

:::tip Lost minus signs
Deviations of classes below the assumed mean are negative.
:::

:::tip f0 and f2 swapped
$f_0$ is before the modal class; $f_2$ is after it.
:::

:::tip Forgetting × h
Step-deviation mean, mode and median all end with "× h".
:::

:::tip No table
Marks are given for the table; show the columns even if you can add mentally.
:::
