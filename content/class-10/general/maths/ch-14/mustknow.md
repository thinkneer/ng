## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Theoretical probability:** favourable outcomes divided by total equally likely outcomes.
- **Equally likely outcomes:** each outcome has the same chance of occurring.
- **Elementary event:** an event with only one outcome.
- **Sure event:** probability 1.
- **Impossible event:** probability 0.
- **Range of probability:** $0\le P(E)\le1$.
- **Complementary event:** "not $E$", with $P(\overline E)=1-P(E)$.
- **Sum of all elementary probabilities:** 1.
- **Pack of cards:** 52 cards, 4 suits of 13.
- **Red cards in a pack:** 26, hearts and diamonds.
- **Black cards in a pack:** 26, spades and clubs.
- **Face cards in a pack:** 12, the jacks, queens and kings.
- **Outcomes for two dice:** 36.
- **Outcomes for three coins:** 8.
- **At least one:** 1 minus the probability of none.

## Key formulas

:::formula Probability of an event
$P(E)=\dfrac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$
:::

:::formula Complementary events
$P(E)+P(\overline E)=1$
:::

:::formula Probability of at least one
$1-P(\text{none})$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Probability of a red queen from a pack: $\dfrac{1}{26}$.
- Probability of a sum of 9 with two dice: $\dfrac19$.
- Probability of a doublet with two dice: $\dfrac16$.
- Most likely sum with two dice: 7.
- Probability of two heads with two coins: $\dfrac14$.
- Probability of a vowel from the letters of MATHEMATICS: $\dfrac{4}{11}$.
- Probability of 53 Sundays in a non-leap year: $\dfrac17$.
- Probability of 53 Sundays in a leap year: $\dfrac27$.
- Which cannot be a probability: a negative number or a number greater than 1.
- If $P(E)=0.37$, then $P(\text{not }E)$: 0.63.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Wrong total
Two dice give 36 outcomes, not 12; three coins give 8, not 6.
:::

:::tip Ace as a face card
Face cards are J, Q, K only: 12 in a pack.
:::

:::tip 1 counted as prime
1 is neither prime nor composite.
:::

:::tip Old total after removal
After removing or adding cards or balls, use the new total.
:::

:::tip Fraction not simplified
Give the probability in lowest terms.
:::
