# Ch 14 · Probability — formulas and key facts

:::formula Probability
$P(E)=\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{total number of equally likely outcomes}}$<br>
$0\le P(E)\le1$ · sure event 1 · impossible event 0<br>
$P(\overline E)=1-P(E)$ · at least one $=1-P(\text{none})$
:::

| Experiment | Total outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 coin · 2 coins · 3 coins | 2 · 4 · 8 |
| 1 die · 2 dice | 6 · 36 |
| 1 card from a pack | 52 |

## The pack of cards

- 4 suits × 13 cards: hearts and diamonds (red, 26), spades and clubs (black, 26).
- Each suit: A, 2–10, J, Q, K. Face cards (J, Q, K): 12. Aces: 4.
