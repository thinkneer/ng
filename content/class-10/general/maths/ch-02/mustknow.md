## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Degree of a polynomial:** the highest power of the variable.
- **Zero of a polynomial:** a value of $x$ for which $p(x)=0$.
- **Zeros on a graph:** the $x$-coordinates where the graph meets the $x$-axis.
- **Maximum number of zeros:** a polynomial of degree $n$ has at most $n$ zeros.
- **Graph of a quadratic polynomial:** a parabola; opens up if $a>0$, down if $a<0$.
- **Zero of a linear polynomial $ax+b$:** $x=-\dfrac ba$.
- **Zeros that are reciprocals:** their product is 1, so $c=a$.
- **Zeros equal and opposite in sign:** their sum is 0, so $b=0$.
- **Not a polynomial:** any expression with $\sqrt x$, $\dfrac1x$ or a negative power of $x$.
- **Not in boards 2026–27:** division algorithm for polynomials.

## Key formulas

:::formula Sum of zeros of a quadratic polynomial
$\alpha+\beta=-\dfrac ba$
:::

:::formula Product of zeros of a quadratic polynomial
$\alpha\beta=\dfrac ca$
:::

:::formula Quadratic polynomial from sum S and product P
$k\,(x^2-Sx+P)$, $k\ne0$
:::

:::formula Sum of squares of the zeros
$\alpha^2+\beta^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta$
:::

:::formula Sum of reciprocals of the zeros
$\dfrac1\alpha+\dfrac1\beta=\dfrac{\alpha+\beta}{\alpha\beta}$
:::

:::formula Square of the difference of the zeros
$(\alpha-\beta)^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-4\alpha\beta$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Number of zeros of a cubic polynomial: at most 3.
- Zeros of $x^2-2x-8$: $4$ and $-2$.
- Quadratic polynomial with zeros $-3$ and $4$: $x^2-x-12$.
- Real zeros of the polynomial $x^2+1$: none.
- Graph touching the $x$-axis at one point only: one zero (two equal zeros).
- If 2 is a zero of $x^2+3x+k$: $k=-10$.
- For $2x^2-8x+6$, sum and product of zeros: $4$ and $3$.
- Graph of a linear polynomial: a straight line with exactly one zero.
- Zeros of $x^2-9$: $3$ and $-3$.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Sign of the sum
Sum of zeros is $-\dfrac ba$, with the minus; product is $\dfrac ca$.
:::

:::tip Middle term of the polynomial
It is $x^2-Sx+P$: minus the sum.
:::

:::tip Verification skipped
"Find and verify" needs both checks written out with the coefficients.
:::

:::tip Counting on the wrong axis
Zeros are where the graph meets the $x$-axis, not the $y$-axis.
:::

:::tip Standard form first
Arrange as $ax^2+bx+c$ before reading $a$, $b$, $c$: $6x^2-3-7x$ has $b=-7$, $c=-3$.
:::
