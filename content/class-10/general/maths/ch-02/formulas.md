# Ch 2 · Polynomials — formulas and key facts

## Basics

- Degree = highest power. Linear $ax+b$ · quadratic $ax^2+bx+c$ · cubic $ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$ ($a\ne0$).
- Zero of $p(x)$: a number $k$ with $p(k)=0$; on the graph, where it meets the $x$-axis.
- A polynomial of degree $n$ has at most $n$ zeros.

## Quadratic polynomial $ax^2+bx+c$ with zeros α, β

:::formula Zeros and coefficients
$\alpha+\beta=-\dfrac ba$   $\alpha\beta=\dfrac ca$<br>
Polynomial with given zeros: $k\left[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta\right]$
:::

| Expression | In terms of $S=\alpha+\beta$, $P=\alpha\beta$ |
|---|---|
| $\alpha^2+\beta^2$ | $S^2-2P$ |
| $(\alpha-\beta)^2$ | $S^2-4P$ |
| $\dfrac1\alpha+\dfrac1\beta$ | $\dfrac SP$ |
| $\alpha^2\beta+\alpha\beta^2$ | $PS$ |

## Graph

| Parabola and the $x$-axis | Number of zeros |
|---|---|
| cuts at two points | 2 |
| touches at one point | 1 (two equal zeros) |
| does not meet | 0 |

Opens upward if $a>0$, downward if $a<0$.
