# Ch 1 · Real Numbers — formulas and key facts

:::formula Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic
Every composite number $=p_1^{a}\,p_2^{b}\cdots$ (primes), in one way only apart from order.
:::

:::formula HCF and LCM
HCF = smallest powers of the **common** primes<br>
LCM = greatest powers of **all** the primes<br>
$\text{HCF}(a,b)\times\text{LCM}(a,b)=a\times b$ (two numbers only)
:::

:::formula Irrationality
If prime $p$ divides $a^2$, then $p$ divides $a$.<br>
$\sqrt p$ is irrational for every prime $p$.<br>
rational ± irrational = irrational; non-zero rational × irrational = irrational
:::
