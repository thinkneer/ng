## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Similar figures:** same shape, not necessarily the same size.
- **Congruent figures:** same shape and same size.
- **Similar triangles:** corresponding angles equal and corresponding sides proportional.
- **Basic Proportionality Theorem:** a line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
- **Converse of BPT:** a line dividing two sides in the same ratio is parallel to the third side.
- **AA similarity test:** two angles of one triangle equal two angles of the other.
- **SSS similarity test:** all three pairs of corresponding sides are proportional.
- **SAS similarity test:** one angle equal and the including sides proportional.
- **Order of letters:** $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle PQR$ pairs $A$–$P$, $B$–$Q$, $C$–$R$.
- **Perimeters of similar triangles:** in the same ratio as corresponding sides.
- **Always similar figures:** all circles, all squares, all equilateral triangles.
- **Area ratio theorem:** not in boards.

## Key formulas

:::formula Basic Proportionality Theorem
$DE\parallel BC\Rightarrow\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{AE}{EC}$
:::

:::formula BPT with whole sides
$\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{DE}{BC}$
:::

:::formula Sides of similar triangles
$\dfrac{AB}{PQ}=\dfrac{BC}{QR}=\dfrac{CA}{RP}$
:::

:::formula Height from a shadow
$\dfrac{\text{height}_1}{\text{shadow}_1}=\dfrac{\text{height}_2}{\text{shadow}_2}$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Two polygons are similar if: angles are equal and sides are proportional.
- All equilateral triangles are: similar.
- All congruent triangles are: similar (the converse is false).
- If $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF$, then $\angle B$ equals: $\angle E$.
- A line through the midpoint of one side, parallel to another: bisects the third side.
- The line joining the midpoints of two sides: is parallel to the third side.
- Triangles with sides 3, 4, 5 and 6, 8, 10: similar by SSS.
- In SAS similarity the equal angle must be: between the proportional sides.
- If sides are in the ratio $2:3$, perimeters are in the ratio: $2:3$.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Parts and wholes mixed
$\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{AE}{EC}$ or $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{AE}{AC}$: never mix a part with a whole.
:::

:::tip DE over BC
$\dfrac{DE}{BC}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}$, not $\dfrac{AD}{DB}$.
:::

:::tip Wrong order of letters
Write the similarity with matching vertices in the same order.
:::

:::tip No reasons
Every equal angle needs its reason in brackets.
:::

:::tip Proof without a figure
Geometry proofs need the figure, Given, To prove and Construction.
:::
