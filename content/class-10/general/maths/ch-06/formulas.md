# Ch 6 · Triangles — formulas and key facts

:::formula Basic Proportionality Theorem and its converse
$DE\parallel BC\iff\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{AE}{EC}$<br>
Also $\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{DE}{BC}$
:::

:::formula Similar triangles
$\triangle ABC\sim\triangle PQR$:<br>
$\angle A=\angle P,\ \angle B=\angle Q,\ \angle C=\angle R$<br>
$\dfrac{AB}{PQ}=\dfrac{BC}{QR}=\dfrac{CA}{RP}=\dfrac{\text{perimeter of }ABC}{\text{perimeter of }PQR}$
:::

| Test | Condition |
|---|---|
| AA | two pairs of equal angles |
| SSS | three pairs of sides proportional |
| SAS | one pair of equal angles, including sides proportional |

- Reasons for equal angles: common angle · vertically opposite · alternate / corresponding (parallel lines) · each 90°.
- Not in boards: ratio of areas of similar triangles; proofs of the Pythagoras theorem and its converse.
