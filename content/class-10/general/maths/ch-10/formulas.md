# Ch 10 · Circles — formulas and key facts

:::formula The two theorems
Tangent ⟂ radius at the point of contact.<br>
Tangents from an external point are equal: $PA=PB$.
:::

:::formula Lengths and angles
Tangent length $=\sqrt{d^2-r^2}$<br>
$\angle APB+\angle AOB=180^\circ$<br>
$OP$ bisects $\angle APB$ and $\angle AOB$<br>
Chord of outer circle touching inner circle $=2\sqrt{R^2-r^2}$
:::

:::formula Circumscribed figures
Quadrilateral: $AB+CD=AD+BC$<br>
Triangle: with $s$ = half the perimeter, the tangent from $A$ is $s-BC$
:::

| Point is | Tangents through it |
|---|---|
| inside the circle | 0 |
| on the circle | 1 |
| outside the circle | 2 |
