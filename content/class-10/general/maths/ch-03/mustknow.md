## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Solution of a pair of equations:** a pair $(x,y)$ that satisfies both equations.
- **Consistent pair:** has at least one solution.
- **Inconsistent pair:** has no solution; the lines are parallel.
- **Dependent pair:** infinitely many solutions; the lines coincide.
- **Graph of a linear equation in two variables:** a straight line.
- **Graphical solution:** the point where the two lines intersect.
- **Substitution method:** express one variable from one equation and put it in the other.
- **Elimination method:** make one variable's coefficients equal, then add or subtract.
- **Two-digit number with digits $x$ and $y$:** $10x+y$; reversed, $10y+x$.
- **Not in boards 2026–27:** cross-multiplication method; equations reducible to linear form.

## Key formulas

:::formula Condition for a unique solution
$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}\ne\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$
:::

:::formula Condition for no solution
$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}=\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}\ne\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$
:::

:::formula Condition for infinitely many solutions
$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}=\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}=\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$
:::

:::formula Speeds of a boat downstream and upstream
downstream $=x+y$, upstream $=x-y$ ($x$ boat in still water, $y$ stream)
:::

:::formula Fixed charge plus rate
total $=x+ny$ ($x$ fixed charge, $y$ rate per unit, $n$ units)
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Lines $x=a$ and $y=b$ meet at: the point $(a,b)$.
- The pair $x+y=5$, $2x+2y=10$: has infinitely many solutions.
- The pair $6x-3y+10=0$, $2x-y+9=0$: has no solution (parallel lines).
- The pair $x+2y=3$, $5x+ky+7=0$ has a unique solution when: $k\ne10$.
- Solution of $x+y=5$, $x-y=1$: $x=3$, $y=2$.
- Intersecting lines mean the pair is: consistent with exactly one solution.
- Coincident lines mean the pair is: consistent with infinitely many solutions.
- A line cuts the $x$-axis where: $y=0$.
- Elimination leaving $0=9$: means no solution.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip Standard form
Bring both equations to the same form before taking ratios.
:::

:::tip Unknowns not defined
Start a word problem with "Let … be $x$ and … be $y$".
:::

:::tip Wrong operation in elimination
Same signs subtract; different signs add.
:::

:::tip Value of k not tested
Check every value of $k$ against the third ratio and reject the wrong one.
:::

:::tip Answer not in words
End with a sentence: "The number is 36", "A pencil costs ₹3".
:::

:::tip Graph without tables or labels
Show the table of points and write each equation beside its line.
:::
