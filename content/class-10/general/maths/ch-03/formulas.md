# Ch 3 · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables — formulas and key facts

General form: $a_1x+b_1y+c_1=0$ and $a_2x+b_2y+c_2=0$.

## Consistency

| Ratios | Lines | Solutions |
|---|---|---|
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2}\ne\frac{b_1}{b_2}$ | intersecting | exactly one |
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{b_1}{b_2}\ne\frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | parallel | none |
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{b_1}{b_2}=\frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | coincident | infinitely many |

Consistent = at least one solution. Inconsistent = no solution.

## Methods

:::formula Solving a pair of linear equations
Graphical: draw both lines; the point of intersection is the solution.<br>
Substitution: make one variable the subject, substitute in the other equation.<br>
Elimination: equalise the coefficients of one variable; same signs subtract, different signs add.
:::

## Word-problem dictionary

| Situation | Expression |
|---|---|
| Two-digit number, digits $x$, $y$ | $10x+y$; reversed $10y+x$ |
| Ages $n$ years ago / later | $x-n$ / $x+n$ |
| Fraction | $\dfrac xy$ |
| Boat and stream | $x+y$ downstream, $x-y$ upstream |
| Fixed charge plus rate | $x+ny$ |
| Distance | speed × time |
