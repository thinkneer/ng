## Must-know lines and definitions

- **Line of sight:** the line from the observer's eye to the object viewed.
- **Angle of elevation:** angle between the line of sight and the horizontal, object above eye level.
- **Angle of depression:** angle between the line of sight and the horizontal, object below eye level.
- **Both angles are measured from:** the horizontal through the observer's eye.
- **Angle of depression from the top:** equals the angle of elevation from the ground point (alternate angles).
- **Height and ground distance:** use the tangent ratio.
- **A slant length (string, ladder, rope):** use sine or cosine.
- **Observer of height 1.5 m:** subtract 1.5 m from the object's height in the triangle.
- **Two objects on the same side:** subtract the two ground distances.
- **Two objects on opposite sides:** add the two ground distances.
- **Moving towards a tower:** the angle of elevation increases.

## Key formulas

:::formula Height from ground distance and angle of elevation
$h=d\tan\theta$
:::

:::formula Ground distance from height and angle
$d=\dfrac{h}{\tan\theta}$
:::

:::formula Height reached by a slant length
$h=l\sin\theta$ (θ with the ground)
:::

:::formula Foot distance of a slant length
$d=l\cos\theta$ (θ with the ground)
:::

:::formula Sun's elevation from a shadow
$\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{height of object}}{\text{length of shadow}}$
:::

:::formula Tangent values used everywhere
$\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3},\ \tan45^\circ=1,\ \tan60^\circ=\sqrt3$
:::

## Quick 1-mark facts (MCQ, A–R)

- Shadow equal to the height of a pole: Sun's elevation is 45°.
- Shadow $\sqrt3$ times the height: Sun's elevation is 30°.
- Height $\sqrt3$ times the shadow: Sun's elevation is 60°.
- A 6 m pole with a shadow of $2\sqrt3$ m: Sun's elevation is 60°.
- Ladder of 15 m making 60° with the wall: reaches 7.5 m up the wall.
- Angle of depression 30° from a 150 m tower: the object is $150\sqrt3$ m from its foot.
- As the Sun rises higher, a shadow: becomes shorter.
- Of two ships seen from a lighthouse, the nearer one: has the larger angle of depression.
- A 20 m rope at 30° to the ground: reaches a height of 10 m.
- Value of $\sqrt3$ to use when asked: 1.732.

## Mark-losing traps

:::tip No figure
A labelled figure carries a mark in every 3- and 5-mark question; draw it first.
:::

:::tip Depression angle in the wrong place
Mark it with the horizontal at the top, then transfer it to the ground.
:::

:::tip Observer's height forgotten
Subtract eye height before using the ratio, or add it back at the end.
:::

:::tip Early decimals
Keep $\sqrt3$ as a surd until the last line.
:::

:::tip Missing unit or sentence
End with a sentence: "The height of the tower is … m."
:::
