# Ch 2 · Shaping of the Earth's Surface — key facts

:::formula Earth's interior
Crust (30–40 km under continents, 5–7 km under oceans) → mantle → outer core (liquid iron–nickel) → inner core (solid). Lithosphere = crust + upper mantle (~100 km); asthenosphere below.
:::

:::formula Plate boundaries
**Convergent:** collide → fold mountains (Himalaya), volcanoes · **Divergent:** apart → new crust (Mid-Atlantic Ridge) · **Transform:** slide past → earthquakes (San Andreas).
:::

:::formula Weathering and erosion
Weathering breaks rock **in place** (physical, chemical, biological) · Erosion **wears away and moves** it.
:::

| Agent | Erosion | Deposition |
|---|---|---|
| Running water | V-valleys, waterfalls, rapids | meanders, oxbow lakes, floodplains, deltas, levees, alluvial fans |
| Waves | cliffs, wave-cut platforms, caves → arches → stacks | beaches, sand bars |
| Glaciers | U-valleys, cirques, arêtes, hanging valleys, fjords | moraines (lateral, medial, terminal) |
| Wind | yardangs, ventifacts, deflation hollows, desert pavements | dunes (barchan, longitudinal, star, parabolic) |
| Groundwater | caves, sinkholes | stalactites, stalagmites, pillars |

:::formula Landform disasters
Landslides · avalanches · GLOFs · sandstorms. Worsened by deforestation, mining, roads, unplanned building. Reduce by hazard maps, early warning, evacuation plans.
:::

:::formula Conservation methods
Contouring · bunding · terracing · check dams · Zabo (Nagaland).
:::
