# Ch 1 · Understanding Social Science — key facts

:::formula What Social Science is
The **systematic** study of human society: what, where, **why** and **how**.
:::

:::formula Four core disciplines
**Geography** (places, environment) · **History** (past, change) · **Political Science** (power, decisions) · **Economics** (limited resources).
:::

:::formula Kinds of historical source
Literary · Archaeological · Epigraphic (inscriptions) · Numismatic (coins) · Scientific (carbon-14, genetics).
:::

:::formula Indian knowledge traditions
Panchamahābhūtas (five elements) · Vasudhaiva Kuṭumbakam (world is one family) · Arthaśāstra (Kauṭilya, ~2,300 years ago) · Itihāsa–Purāṇa.
:::

| Discipline | Asks | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| Geography | Where, and why there? | maps, globes, atlases, GIS, fieldwork |
| History | When, and what changed? | literary, archaeological, epigraphic, numismatic, scientific sources |
| Political Science | Who decides, and how? | constitutions, laws, policies, debates |
| Economics | How are limited resources used? | data on prices, production, incomes; surveys |
